Why "LA Retail Rent" Isn't a Single Number

Los Angeles retail spans a wider basis range than almost any other property type in the market: a Fashion District showroom, a UCLA-adjacent food-service storefront, and a Rodeo Drive flagship all sit under the same "retail" label but trade on entirely different economics. Corridor-driven retail (Melrose, Larchmont, Ventura Boulevard, the Golden Triangle) prices off foot traffic, brand cachet, and tenant credit; grocery-anchored centers and auto/QSR pad sites price off daily-needs demand durability and drive-through economics instead. Pulling a citywide average rent or cap rate across all of it obscures more than it reveals.

Conditional use permit review for alcohol sales or late-hour operation shows up across nearly every corridor on this list, and it is worth budgeting into a leasing or acquisition timeline regardless of submarket. Beyond that, each corridor below has its own zoning authority, tenant profile, and lender pool, pick your corridor for the specifics rather than assuming one retail underwriting model fits all of LA.
