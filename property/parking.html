Parking Facility and Garage Financing

Parking facilities serve a unique role in commercial real estate financing, with cash flow dynamics that combine real estate, infrastructure, and operating-business characteristics. Commercial Lending Solutions arranges financing for urban parking garages, surface lots, airport parking, transit-oriented facilities, and mixed-use parking podiums from banks, CMBS conduits, life insurance companies, and specialty parking lenders who understand the asset class.

Overview

Parking Facility and Garage Financing

Parking financing splits into two categories: pure-play standalone garages and surface lots underwritten on parking revenue alone, and parking podiums embedded in mixed-use or office buildings underwritten as part of the larger asset. Standalone parking is harder to finance than other CRE asset classes because lenders treat it as part real estate, part operating business. Revenue is sensitive to occupancy patterns of nearby buildings, transit ridership, and special events, all of which can be volatile. Despite this, well-located urban garages in transit-rich downtowns, dense employment centers, and airport-adjacent submarkets attract reliable capital from banks, life insurance companies on triple-net ground leases to credit-tenant operators, CMBS conduits, and specialty parking REITs. Surface lots in high-density urban settings command premium pricing as redevelopment land plays. Commercial Lending Solutions has placed financing across standalone garages, surface lots, airport parking, ground-lease structures, and mixed-use parking components.
