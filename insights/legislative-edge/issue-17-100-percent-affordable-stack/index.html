01 The Incentive  the law, and who qualifies

Go 100% affordable near transit, and the state gets out of your way.

Under the state density bonus law, a 100% affordable project near a major transit stop gets no density cap and no parking requirement. LA's AHIP program makes it ministerial: no hearing, no CEQA. Add a nonprofit partner and the welfare exemption can take property tax off the table too.
Hollywood/Highland, 0.33 miles from the site.
Does your site qualify?
✓Within half a mile of a major transit stop
✓Zoned for multifamily, five or more units
✓Nothing rent stabilized to replace (vacant is easiest)
✓All but the manager's unit affordable, most at 80% AMI or less

In effect now. Sources: Gov. Code 65915, LA AHIP (Ord. 188477), Rev. and Tax. Code 214.

03 The Numbers  what the land is really worth

Without the incentive, this lot is worth less than nothing.

Start with what the finished building is worth, subtract every cost
The seller is asking $3.995M. Land values are break even, after every cost and the interest on the loan.

I'll be blunt. New construction in LA costs about what the rents will support, before you pay a dollar for land. So the real question is what the dirt is worth. With the stack, about $2.15 million to break even. Without it, nothing.

04 The Financing  how I'd put the debt together

How I'd finance it (at a $1.5 million purchase)

The capital stack, $25.75M total cost
Loan 63% Equity 37%
$16.29M construction loan $9.47M your equity
Term sheet
Construction loan$16.29M
Loan to cost63% (lenders go up to 75%)
RateSOFR + 3.00%, about 6.87% today
Term29 months, interest only
Sized by1.20x coverage at 6.5%, 30 year amortization
Perm takeout$16.29M, pays off the construction loan in full

Lenders will go up to 75% of cost, but on a deal like this the income sets the loan: 1.20x coverage at 6.5% on a 30 year amortization. That's why it lands at 63%. The upside is that the perm pays it off in full, so there's no surprise at conversion. First, though, lenders want your nonprofit partner signed and a contractor who has built micro units at this cost.
