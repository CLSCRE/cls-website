Overview

DSCR Loans for Real Estate Investors

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) lending evaluates a property on one simple question: does the gross rental income cover the debt service? If the property cash flows, the loan qualifies. This structure eliminates the need for tax returns, W-2s, pay stubs, or traditional income documentation, making DSCR a powerful tool for high-net-worth investors, self-employed borrowers, and portfolio landlords scaling their holdings. Typical DSCR thresholds range from 1.10 to 1.25, meaning the property generates 10 to 25 percent more income than the loan payment. Lenders include dedicated non-QM platforms, balance-sheet debt funds, and select private lenders with appetite for investor residential and small commercial assets.

  • Self-employed borrowers with significant write-offs
  • Portfolio landlords consolidating scattered properties
  • 1031 exchange acquisitions needing fast income qualification

DSCR Loans FAQ

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies the borrower based on the property's rental income rather than personal income or W-2 earnings. Lenders divide the property's net operating income by the annual debt service to calculate the DSCR. A ratio above 1.0x means the property generates enough cash flow to cover its mortgage payment. DSCR loans are ideal for self-employed investors, real estate professionals, and anyone with complex income who owns strong income-producing properties.
Most DSCR lenders require a minimum ratio of 1.20x to 1.25x, meaning the property must generate 20 to 25 percent more income than its monthly mortgage payment. Some lenders offer DSCR programs as low as 1.0x (break-even) or even no-DSCR programs for strong borrowers, though rates are higher at lower ratios. Commercial Lending Solutions can access DSCR programs across the full ratio spectrum.
DSCR loan rates currently range from 6.50% to 9.00% depending on the loan-to-value ratio, property type, borrower credit profile, and DSCR ratio. Rates have compressed from 2023 peaks as lenders compete aggressively for investor-grade DSCR volume. Contact Commercial Lending Solutions for a current rate quote specific to your property and market.
DSCR loans close in 10 to 21 days on average, significantly faster than conventional bank loans that require 45 to 90 days. Streamlined income documentation (rental income verification rather than full tax return analysis) enables the faster timeline. This speed makes DSCR ideal for competitive acquisitions where sellers prefer a quick close.

Lenders typically use market rent or 70 to 80 percent of gross STR revenue for qualifying income. Some lenders specifically target STR properties given the higher income potential relative to long-term rentals.
A conventional investment loan qualifies the borrower using personal income, tax returns, W-2s, and a debt-to-income ratio. A DSCR loan qualifies based solely on the property's rental income relative to its debt service. DSCR loans require no income documentation from the borrower, no employment verification, and no DTI calculation, making them ideal for self-employed investors and those with complex financials who own strong cash-flowing properties.
