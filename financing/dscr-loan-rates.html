What Drives DSCR Loan Rates

Understanding these factors helps you position your deal for the best available rate.

DSCR Ratio

The coverage ratio is the primary underwriting criterion. A DSCR of 1.25x or higher unlocks the best rates. Every 0.05x drop in DSCR below 1.25x typically adds 25 to 50 basis points to the rate.

Loan-to-Value Ratio

Lower leverage means lower rate. Moving from 75% to 65% LTV can reduce your rate by 50 to 100 basis points. Most lenders cap DSCR loans at 80% LTV.

Credit Score

Unlike conventional loans, DSCR lenders still look at borrower credit. A 740+ score unlocks the best pricing. Scores below 680 typically add 75 to 150 bps to the rate.

Property Type

Single-family rentals and small multifamily (2 to 4 units) get the tightest rates. Short-term rentals (Airbnb, VRBO) are accepted by many lenders but add a risk premium of 25 to 50 bps.

Loan Amount

DSCR loans above $1.5M move into jumbo territory and can see slightly higher rates. Loans under $150K may also carry rate premiums due to fixed origination costs.

Treasury Benchmarks

DSCR rates are priced as a spread over 5, 7, or 10-year US Treasury yields. When Treasuries rise, DSCR rates rise proportionally. Rate locks at application protect against this risk.

DSCR Loan Rates 2026: Frequently Asked Questions

What are current DSCR loan rates?

DSCR loan rates in May 2026 range from 6.50% to 9.00% for investment properties. The rate depends on your DSCR ratio, LTV, credit score, and property type. A strong deal with DSCR above 1.25x and 65% LTV can qualify for rates starting around 6.50%. Lower coverage ratios and higher leverage push rates toward 9.00%.

What DSCR ratio gets the best rate?

A DSCR of 1.25x or higher qualifies for the best rate tier from most lenders, typically 6.50% to 7.50%. Some lenders offer programs down to 1.0x (breakeven) but at higher rates of 8.25% to 9.00%. No-DSCR programs for strong asset profiles exist at 8.50% to 9.50%.

Are DSCR loan rates fixed or adjustable?

Most DSCR loans are structured as fixed-rate loans with a 5, 7, or 10-year fixed period followed by a balloon payment or rate adjustment. Fully fixed 30-year DSCR loans are available from select lenders. The most common structure is a 30-year amortization with a 5 or 7-year fixed rate and prepayment penalty.

Do DSCR loans have prepayment penalties?

Yes. Most DSCR loans carry step-down prepayment penalties (typically 5-4-3-2-1 for a 5-year fixed) or yield maintenance provisions. The penalty period matches the fixed-rate term. Lenders offering the lowest rates typically require the longest prepayment protection.

Can I use DSCR financing for short-term rentals?

Yes. Many DSCR lenders in May 2026 accept short-term rental income verified through Airbnb or VRBO platform statements, property management reports, or market rent surveys. STR income may be calculated at 70% to 80% of gross revenue for qualifying purposes. Rates are typically 25 to 50 basis points higher than for long-term rentals.
