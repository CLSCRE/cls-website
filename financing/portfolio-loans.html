Blanket Portfolio Loans for Real Estate Investors

Portfolio loans allow real estate investors to finance multiple properties under a single loan facility, replacing individual property mortgages with one streamlined structure. Commercial Lending Solutions sources portfolio financing from balance-sheet lenders, debt funds, and specialty platforms that underwrite on the performance of the full portfolio rather than each asset individually, simplifying management and often unlocking better terms than property-by-property financing.

Overview

Blanket Portfolio Loans for Real Estate Investors

As investors scale their holdings, managing individual loans across dozens of properties becomes operationally complex and expensive. A blanket portfolio loan consolidates these into one facility with a single lender, one payment, and unified loan covenants. Underwriting focuses on overall portfolio DSCR, blended occupancy, and total equity rather than the specific characteristics of each property. This structure is especially powerful for investors who hold a mix of asset types, have properties in multiple states, or own properties that individually would not qualify for agency or bank financing. Commercial Lending Solutions has relationships with portfolio lenders that specialize in this product and understand how to structure cross-collateralized facilities for maximum flexibility and borrower leverage.

Lender Sources

  • Institutional Balance-Sheet Lenders
  • Debt Funds
  • Private Banks
  • Family Offices
  • Insurance Company Portfolio Programs

Ideal For

  • Institutional investors with 10 to 100+ properties seeking one facility
  • Mixed-asset portfolios spanning multifamily, industrial, and retail
  • Sponsors retiring multiple individual loans at maturity into one execution
  • Private equity and family office real estate portfolios
  • Out-of-state investors with geographically diversified holdings
  • Operators seeking to recapitalize and extract equity across a portfolio
