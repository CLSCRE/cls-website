Industrial Construction Financing

Industrial Construction Loans for Warehouse and Logistics Development

Industrial construction loans fund ground-up warehouse, distribution, flex industrial, and cold storage development projects. These 18 to 36-month facilities are structured for industrial development: phased construction draws, pre-lease or speculative underwriting, and exit to CMBS, life company, or agency permanent financing at stabilization. Minimum loan size is $2 million, targeting industrial developments in major logistics corridors.

Overview

Construction Financing for Warehouse, Logistics, and Industrial Development

Industrial and logistics construction remains one of the most active development sectors, driven by persistent e-commerce demand, nearshoring and reshoring of manufacturing, and constrained infill land supply in primary distribution markets. Lenders evaluate industrial construction on market absorption data, sponsor track record in industrial development, pre-leasing percentage, and exit cap rate assumptions. Speculative industrial construction in supply-constrained markets continues to attract bank and debt fund capital. Pre-leased build-to-suit industrial attracts the most competitive terms given the credit-tenant lease backing the project.

Lender Sources

  • Banks
  • Debt Funds
  • Life Insurance Companies (Pre-Leased)
  • Private Lenders
  • CDFI Lenders

Ideal For

  • Speculative warehouse and distribution construction in high-absorption logistics markets
  • Build-to-suit industrial for credit tenants including e-commerce, 3PL, and manufacturing
  • Cold storage and temperature-controlled warehouse construction
  • Last-mile infill industrial redevelopment and new construction
  • Flex industrial and light manufacturing park development
  • Industrial portfolio developers building multiple facilities under a single facility
