Overview

Financing for Self-Storage Facilities and Climate-Controlled Storage

Self-storage has outperformed nearly every other commercial real estate asset class over the past two decades, driven by demographic mobility, apartment downsizing trends, and low operating costs relative to revenue. Cap rates have compressed to 4.5-6.5% for Class A facilities in primary markets, attracting institutional capital including REITs, private equity, and life companies. The asset class is particularly resilient in downturns because both personal and business storage demand holds up during economic stress. New supply has been active in secondary and tertiary markets, creating opportunities for ground-up development alongside acquisition plays. Climate-controlled and Class A facilities in high-traffic suburban locations command the best financing terms.

Lender Sources

  • CMBS Conduits
  • Life Insurance Companies
  • Banks
  • SBA 7(a) and 504
  • Debt Funds

Ideal For

  • Climate-controlled self-storage facility acquisitions in high-density suburban markets
  • Self-storage ground-up construction in supply-constrained corridors
  • Boat and RV storage facilities near coastal and recreational areas
  • Multi-story urban infill self-storage in dense metros
  • Portfolio acquisitions of multiple self-storage facilities
  • Conversion of retail, industrial, or office buildings into self-storage
