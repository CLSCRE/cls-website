Overview

Commercial Land Acquisition and Pre-Development Financing

Land financing is the highest-risk category in commercial real estate lending because collateral value depends entirely on entitlement outcomes and future development feasibility. Conventional banks rarely lend on unentitled or partially entitled land, leaving the market to private lenders, debt funds, and family offices with appetite for development risk. Key underwriting factors include entitlement status, sponsor track record, comparable development sales, market absorption data, and the path to a bankable construction loan. Entitled land with approved plans and permits commands significantly better terms than raw land. Deals in supply-constrained markets with documented developer experience are the most competitive.

Lender Sources

  • Private Lenders
  • Debt Funds
  • Hard Money Lenders
  • Family Offices
  • Specialty Land Banks

Ideal For

  • Entitled residential and commercial land acquisitions ready for construction start
  • Partially entitled land while entitlement process is completed
  • Lot acquisition for homebuilders with active absorption in the submarket
  • Industrial and logistics land in high-demand corridors near major distribution hubs
  • Infill land assemblage in urban markets for mixed-use development
  • Agricultural and transitional land being repositioned for commercial development
