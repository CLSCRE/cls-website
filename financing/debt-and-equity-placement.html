What debt placement means

Debt placement is the work of taking a financing request, deciding which type of lender should fund it, and winning the best executable proposal from the lenders who actually want that deal.

It rests on three things: an honest underwriting of the property and the sponsor, current knowledge of which lender desks have appetite right now, and disciplined execution from term sheet to funding. Our team came from the debt and structured finance groups at CBRE, Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation, Genesis Capital and Slatt Capital, and our aggregate career volume exceeds $1 billion across property types and markets.

Where the capital comes from

We do not treat our lender list as a directory. We approach the desks whose current appetite fits the asset, the sponsor, the market and the business plan, and we compare the full execution, not only the rate.

Banks and credit unions

Relationship capital with local market knowledge and structural flexibility, with recourse often part of the discussion.

Life insurance companies

Long-duration fixed-rate capital for high quality assets and experienced sponsors, generally at conservative leverage. We correspond directly with life companies.

CMBS conduits

Non-recourse execution with broad property appetite, balanced against standardized servicing and prepayment terms.

Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and HUD/FHA

Programmatic multifamily and housing capital with defined underwriting, documentation and eligibility requirements.

Debt funds

Flexible capital for transitional, complex, leveraged or time-sensitive executions where a conventional credit box does not fit.

Private lenders

Private debt for transitional, time-sensitive and higher leverage executions.

How a placement works

Every financing follows the same sequence. The full seven steps, with what we do and what we need from you at each one, are on our commercial loan process page.

  1. 1

    Underwrite the opportunity

    We build our own underwriting of the property and the sponsor before approaching any lender, so we know what the asset supports and where the weak points are.

  2. 2

    Agree the structure

    We walk you through the options and an indicative view of terms, so you know the likely path before we go to market.

  3. 3

    Take it to the right lenders

    We present a complete loan package to the desks whose current appetite fits and follow up on any that go quiet.

  4. 4

    Compare and close

    Proposals are compared on rate, proceeds, recourse, reserves, prepayment and closing certainty. Once you choose, we manage application, third-party reports and closing until the loan funds.

What a placement advisor adds

  • A comparable field. Banks, life companies, CMBS conduits, agency lenders and debt funds each read the same rent roll differently. We put them in front of the same deal so you can compare real proposals instead of accepting the first term sheet.
  • Structure before pricing. Leverage, recourse, reserves, prepayment and closing certainty can matter as much as the coupon. We test those trade-offs before recommending a path.
  • The right structure. Where a conventional loan does not fit, we test other debt structures rather than stopping at the first no.
  • Execution through closing. We coordinate lender diligence, third-party reports, title and issue resolution until the loan funds, with a weekly update while the deal is in flight.
  • An early, honest answer. If a deal does not fit a given capital source, we say so early and tell you which market we would run it to instead.

Frequently asked questions

What is commercial real estate debt placement?

It is the work of taking a financing request, deciding which type of lender should fund it, and securing the best executable proposal from the lenders whose current appetite fits. We place permanent, construction, bridge, life company, CMBS and agency loans, and we manage the loan from first conversation through funding.

Is Commercial Lending Solutions a lender?

No. CLS CRE is a commercial mortgage broker and correspondent. We do not lend our own funds and are never the lender of record. The loan is made by the lender you select from the proposals we obtain for you.

What size financing do you place?

We place financing from $2 million to $100 million and up. Loans under $2 million are outside our minimum loan size.

How many lenders do you work with, and do you send my deal to all of them?

We have relationships with more than 1,000 lenders and capital providers, but we do not send a deal to all of them. We target the desks whose current appetite fits the property, the sponsor and the structure, so that the proposals you receive are real choices rather than noise.

How quickly will I hear back, and how fast can a loan close?

We respond to a financing request within 24 hours with an initial view. Bridge loans can close in as little as 7 business days and construction loans in under 30 days, subject to the lender and a complete document package. Permanent loans typically take 45 to 90 days. The full sequence is laid out step by step on our commercial loan process page.

Will I get updates while my loan is in process?

Yes. Every placement in process gets a weekly update covering where the loan is, what the lender asked for last, what is due this week and any movement in pricing or structure.

Have a financing request from $2 million to $100 million and up?

Share the property and what you want to finance. We respond within 24 hours with an initial view of the structure and the capital sources that fit.
