I am reaching out because we work with nonprofit affordable housing developers across California, and I wanted to share a resource that has helped several of our clients significantly improve their project economics.

We published a free guide on California's Welfare Exemption (Revenue and Taxation Code Section 214), the provision that allows qualifying nonprofit-owned affordable housing to receive a full property tax exemption.

On a $20M project, the Welfare Exemption saves $220,000 to $250,000 per year in property taxes. That improved NOI strengthens your DSCR and can unlock better financing terms.

The guide covers:

Who qualifies -- 501(c)(3) nonprofits, housing authorities, and tribal organizations
Application process -- step-by-step BOE filing with deadlines
Financing strategies -- pairing the exemption with LIHTC, tax-exempt bonds, and CDFI loans
Real numbers -- how the tax savings flow through to your pro forma and lender underwriting
