Commercial Real Estate Financing in Oak Brook

Oak Brook sits at the I-88/I-294 interchange in DuPage County and built its commercial identity as a corporate headquarters address, most notably as the longtime home of McDonald's global headquarters before the company relocated downtown to Chicago's West Loop in 2018. Ace Hardware remains headquartered in Oak Brook today, and the village's office stock still carries the imprint of that corporate-campus era: low-rise, amenity-heavy suburban office product built for single large tenants rather than multi-tenant flexibility. That legacy has created one of the more interesting office repositioning stories in the Chicago suburbs, as sponsors acquire former single-tenant campuses and reposition them for multi-tenant, medical, or alternative use.

Retail is Oak Brook's other defining commercial asset class, anchored by Oakbrook Center, one of the largest and highest-performing super-regional malls in the Midwest. The mall's continued strength, driven by a luxury and upper-mid tenant mix that has outperformed weaker regional malls elsewhere, supports a healthy ecosystem of adjacent retail, restaurant, and lifestyle-center financing along 22nd Street and Route 83.

Active Property Types and Typical Deal Sizes

Office repositioning and value-add acquisition is our most active Oak Brook category, typically ranging from $10 million to $45 million for former single-tenant corporate campus buildings being converted to multi-tenant, medical office, or flex use. These deals require lenders comfortable underwriting to a post-repositioning stabilized cash flow, often with a meaningful capital improvement budget for lobby, mechanical, and amenity upgrades.

Retail financing adjacent to Oakbrook Center, both mall-adjacent outparcels and nearby lifestyle centers, typically runs $5 million to $25 million and benefits from strong comparable sales given the mall's performance. Medical office and healthcare financing has grown as a category as former office tenants are replaced by outpatient and specialty medical users, generally in the $3 million to $15 million range. Hotel financing along the I-88 corridor, supporting both corporate travel and Oakbrook Center visitors, typically falls between $8 million and $30 million for select-service and full-service properties.

The Lender Ecosystem for Oak Brook Deals

Debt funds and bridge lenders dominate the office repositioning category, since conventional banks generally will not underwrite former single-tenant campus product without a clear, de-risked multi-tenant leasing plan already in place. CMBS conduits and life insurance companies remain the most competitive source for stabilized, well-leased retail near Oakbrook Center, particularly where national credit tenants anchor the rent roll.

Regional banks with DuPage County branch presence are active on smaller medical office and owner-occupied deals under $10 million, while hospitality financing typically flows through a mix of regional banks and specialty hotel lenders familiar with the I-88 corridor's demand drivers. We run a competitive process across all of these categories on every Oak Brook assignment rather than defaulting to a single lender type.

Oak Brook Market Considerations

The office repositioning opportunity in Oak Brook is real, but it is basis-dependent: sponsors who acquire former corporate campus product at a low enough price per square foot to fund a genuine multi-tenant or medical conversion are seeing strong returns, while those underwriting to legacy single-tenant rents are not. We push every Oak Brook office sponsor to build a conservative, comp-supported re-tenanting timeline before we take a deal to lenders.

DuPage County's commercial tax structure applies here as it does across the western suburbs, and Oak Brook's village-level incentive programs for office redevelopment are worth exploring on larger repositioning deals, since they can materially improve project economics when layered correctly into the capital stack.
