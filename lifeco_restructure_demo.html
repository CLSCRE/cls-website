Life company lending

Life Company Loans for Commercial Real Estate

Life company loans range from $5M to $100M+ at 5.00% to 6.50% fixed, typically 10 to 25 years, up to about 65% LTV, non-recourse. Desks quote spreads over Treasury: tight execution runs 120 to 180 bps, so apply the current bond yield to estimate your rate. Best for institutional multifamily, industrial, and credit net lease in primary markets. Commercial Lending Solutions accesses the full life company market through two channels: direct open-market desks (New York Life, Northwestern Mutual, MetLife, Manulife, PGIM) and correspondent programs — see the named lender table below.
