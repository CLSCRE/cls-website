Commercial Real Estate Loans in Indiana

Reviewed by Trevor Damyan , Commercial Mortgage Broker · Updated August 2026
Quick answer: Commercial Lending Solutions arranges commercial real estate loans across Indiana from $1 million to over $100 million, spanning 42 loan programs and every major property type. We maintain dedicated market coverage for 4 Indiana metros, including Evansville and Fort Wayne. Below: how Indiana's foreclosure process, recording taxes, and regulatory climate shape the loan terms lenders will offer here.

Indiana commercial real estate financing benefits from one of the most predictable cost structures in the Midwest, and lenders reward that predictability with real appetite. Commercial Lending Solutions arranges commercial real estate loans across Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, Evansville, and South Bend. Indianapolis sits at the Crossroads of America, where I-65, I-70, I-69, and I-74 converge, and its logistics economy is anchored by the FedEx Express national hub at Indianapolis International Airport, the second largest in that carrier's global network, with big-box distribution concentrated in Plainfield, Whitestown, and Greenwood. Eli Lilly's multibillion dollar buildout at the LEAP Research and Innovation District northwest of Indianapolis is pulling life sciences and advanced manufacturing capital into central Indiana at a scale few Midwest states can match. Fort Wayne runs on diversified manufacturing and defense electronics, with the orthopedic device capital of Warsaw nearby; Evansville anchors the state's southwest with plastics, healthcare, and a Toyota assembly plant in the metro's orbit; and South Bend pairs the University of Notre Dame with one of the largest hyperscale data center projects in the country rising in nearby New Carlisle.

Capital flows into Indiana because the math is stable. Constitutional property tax caps hold commercial property to a fixed share of assessed value, assessments are predictable, and entitlement is fast, so lenders can underwrite expenses with confidence they rarely get next door in Illinois. Agency lenders and regional banks compete hard for Indianapolis multifamily, life companies chase the I-70 industrial corridor, and CLS CRE runs that competition across 1,000+ capital sources to make sure Indiana borrowers see it in their term sheets.

Foreclosure and Lender Appetite

Indiana foreclosures go through the courts, with uncontested commercial cases typically resolving in well under a year and contested ones running longer. The judicial path adds some recovery time versus trustee-sale states, so a few lenders trim leverage on transitional deals, but Indiana's stable costs keep the overall lender pool deep.

Recording Taxes and Closing Costs

Indiana charges no mortgage recording tax, only standard county recording fees, so refinances and recapitalizations are cheap to execute.

Indiana is one of the easier states in the country to bring to market. Property tax caps make expense underwriting unusually reliable, land and construction costs run below national averages, and the state's incentive toolkit for industrial and data center projects is aggressive. Lenders like Indianapolis multifamily and I-70 corridor industrial, and the Lilly LEAP district has put construction lenders on alert for supplier and housing demand in Boone County. Secondary markets like Fort Wayne, Evansville, and South Bend are relationship-bank territory where local deposits and guarantor strength drive terms more than asset gloss.

Where Deals Get Done

Key Commercial Real Estate Sectors in Indiana

Industrial and Logistics

Plainfield and Whitestown rank among the most active big-box distribution submarkets in the Midwest, fed by the FedEx hub at Indianapolis International and four interstates converging on the metro. Banks, life companies, and debt funds all quote the corridor.

Life Sciences and Advanced Manufacturing

Eli Lilly's LEAP district investment is among the largest pharmaceutical manufacturing commitments in US history, and Warsaw's orthopedic cluster gives northern Indiana a durable medical device base. Both feed demand for flex, lab, and supplier industrial space.

Multifamily

Indianapolis apartments draw steady agency and regional bank competition, with suburban Hamilton County product commanding the tightest execution. College-anchored demand in South Bend and steady employment in Fort Wayne support secondary-market deals.

Data Centers

The hyperscale buildout in New Carlisle near South Bend and additional campuses across central Indiana reflect cheap land, available power, and state incentives, creating construction lending and adjacent industrial demand.

Market Intelligence

Regulatory Environment

Indiana is a genuinely light-touch state and capital knows it. Constitutional property tax caps limit commercial property to 3 percent of assessed value, which makes the expense line one of the most predictable in the country. There is no rent control, landlord-tenant law is owner-friendly, and permitting in most jurisdictions moves quickly, with counties competing for industrial and data center projects through abatements and tax increment financing. Utility power availability has become the main gating item for large projects rather than zoning. For lenders, the practical effect is confidence: underwritten expenses hold, entitlement risk is low, and construction deals pencil with fewer contingencies than in coastal or high-tax markets.

Which Lenders Are Active in Indiana

Regional banks headquartered around the Midwest treat Indiana as a core market, and community banks in Fort Wayne, Evansville, and South Bend lend confidently on local collateral. Agency lenders are aggressive on Indianapolis and suburban multifamily, life insurance companies pursue distribution product along I-70 and I-65, and debt funds price value-add deals in the metro competitively. Credit unions have grown their commercial books statewide. CMBS shows up for stabilized retail and hospitality in secondary markets. It is a deep, rational lender pool, and well-packaged deals get multiple serious quotes.
