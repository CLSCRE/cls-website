Life Company Self Storage Lending in Des Moines

Des Moines occupies a unique position in the life company capital markets landscape. Principal Financial Group, EMC Insurance, and other institutional capital allocators are headquartered here, which means local commercial real estate has long benefited from a level of institutional familiarity that secondary markets rarely enjoy. Life company underwriters who deploy capital into Des Moines industrial, multifamily, and net lease product are not working from a market summary report. They know the I-35 and I-80 corridors, they understand submarket dynamics in West Des Moines and Ankeny, and they have watched the greater metro absorb economic cycles with consistent stability. That institutional comfort now extends to self storage, a sector that has earned a permanent place in life company allocation targets over the past decade.

Self storage in Des Moines benefits from the same demographic and economic fundamentals driving the broader metro. The suburban ring, including Ankeny, Johnston, Urbandale, and Clive, has added households steadily, and the population growth in those corridors has created durable demand for climate-controlled personal and business storage. Life companies evaluating Des Moines self storage deals are not speculating on emerging demand. They are underwriting stabilized cash flows in markets they already monitor for multifamily and NNN retail allocations. That overlap matters. An institution already holding a West Des Moines apartment loan or an I-80 logistics facility has existing market intelligence that supports faster, more confident underwriting on a climate-controlled self storage asset two miles away.

The competitive dynamic in Des Moines self storage also works in favor of institutional financing. Unlike oversupplied coastal markets where lease-up timelines have stretched and occupancy has come under pressure, the Des Moines suburban submarkets have absorbed new supply at a measured pace. Life companies target stabilized facilities, and Des Moines has produced a reliable inventory of them. Institutional-quality operators running climate-controlled facilities in infill suburban locations with 85 percent or better occupancy are exactly what life company capital is built to finance.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Des Moines Self Storage Deals

Life company underwriting criteria for self storage in Des Moines are consistent with the broader program parameters, applied with close attention to local market conditions. On leverage, life companies are pricing at 55 to 65 percent of stabilized value as a standard range. This is not a soft ceiling. Underwriters are disciplined on LTV because the non-recourse structure requires the collateral to carry the loan across a 10 to 20 year fixed term without sponsor support. Deals that arrive at or below 60 percent LTV receive the most favorable execution. Sponsors who need to stretch above 65 percent will find life company capital less competitive than other sources.

Debt service coverage requirements typically run in the 1.30x to 1.40x range on a stressed basis, and underwriters apply their own vacancy and expense load rather than accepting operator projections at face value. For Des Moines facilities, that means underwriters will stress occupancy toward market historical averages and apply institutional expense ratios regardless of how efficiently a particular operator runs the asset. DSCR requirements are not negotiated down based on sponsor relationships or market narrative.

On rate, the current environment with the 10-year Treasury in the range of 4.3 percent and life company spreads for stabilized self storage generally running 125 to 175 basis points above the index produces all-in fixed rates that remain competitive with alternative permanent financing sources. Climate-controlled facilities in infill suburban locations attract tighter spread execution. Life companies have embraced self storage as a viable alternative to office and retail allocations, and that increased demand for the product type has kept pricing disciplined. Amortization schedules run 25 to 30 years, and prepayment provisions are structured as yield maintenance or a declining step schedule, which aligns with the long-hold investment profile this financing is designed to serve.

The Direct Correspondent Advantage

The borrower has no direct relationship with the decision-maker, the broker cannot commit to terms, and the approval clock starts over every time the file moves to a new reviewer. Timelines of 75 to 120 days or more are common in that model, and the sponsor never knows exactly where the deal sits in the approval queue.

As a direct correspondent lender, Commercial Lending Solutions underwrites self storage transactions in-house and issues commitments in our own name. There is no submission to a home office, no unknown underwriter on the other end of the file, and no third-party approval layer sitting between the borrower and the credit decision. Our underwriter is the underwriter. When we issue a term sheet, it reflects our own underwriting conclusion, not a pre-qualification estimate we are hoping a life company's credit committee will ratify. That structure compresses the timeline to 45 to 75 days from LOI to closing for well-prepared deals, and it allows us to have substantive conversations about deal structure before a file is formally submitted anywhere. For sponsors who have worked through the indirect placement process, the operational difference is material.

Typical Des Moines Life Company Self Storage Deal Profile

A representative life company self storage transaction in the Des Moines market falls in the range of $5 million to $20 million, though the program supports deals from $3 million up to $50 million for the right asset. The property is typically a purpose-built, climate-controlled facility constructed within the past 10 to 20 years, located in an infill suburban submarket such as Ankeny, West Des Moines, Johnston, or Clive. Drive-up only facilities in exurban locations attract less institutional interest. Climate-controlled urban and suburban infill is the preferred profile.

Occupancy requirements are firm. Underwriters want to see sustained occupancy at or above 85 percent, supported by operating history of at least 24 months at stabilized levels. A facility that hit 85 percent six months ago is a different underwriting conversation than one that has operated at 88 to 92 percent for three years. Sponsors are typically experienced operators or institutional ownership groups, often with multiple facilities under management. Single-asset sponsors with a strong financial profile and clean title history can qualify, but the underwriting scrutiny on the sponsorship side is real and should not be underestimated.

From LOI to closing, a direct correspondent execution on a prepared file runs 45 to 75 days. That assumes third-party reports are ordered promptly after LOI acceptance, title is clean, and the borrower is responsive. Indirect broker placements to life company home offices regularly run 90 to 120 days or longer, and timeline slippage is common when underwriting questions cycle back through an intermediary.

Life Company versus Other Permanent Options in Des Moines

Life company financing is not the right answer for every Des Moines self storage deal, and it is worth being direct about where it wins and where other capital sources are more appropriate.

Life company execution is most competitive for stabilized, low-leverage deals with sponsors who intend to hold long-term. The fixed rate, non-recourse structure, and 10 to 20 year term are well matched to institutional operators or private equity groups with a defined long-hold investment thesis. For a $10 million climate-controlled facility in Ankeny at 60 percent LTV with 90 percent occupancy and three years of operating history, life company capital is likely to produce the best combination of rate, structure, and certainty.

CMBS can compete on rate at similar leverage points but comes with more restrictive cash management requirements, less flexibility on assumptions, and servicer relationships that some sponsors find operationally burdensome. Bank balance sheet financing offers more flexibility on structure and sometimes higher leverage, but floating rates and 5 to 7 year terms with extension risk are not a match for sponsors seeking long-term fixed certainty. Debt funds are the right answer for value-add deals, lease-up situations, or sponsors who need leverage above 65 percent. Life companies do not compete for those deals and are not designed to. Agency financing is specific to multifamily and is not relevant to self storage. For stabilized infill self storage in Des Moines with a long-term hold horizon and a sponsor who can operate within a 55 to 65 percent LTV constraint, life company capital is the most efficient permanent financing solution in the market today.
