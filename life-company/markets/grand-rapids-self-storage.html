Life Company Self Storage Lending in Grand Rapids, MI

Grand Rapids is not a primary life company market in the way that Chicago, Dallas, or Atlanta command outsized allocations, but institutional capital has found its footing here, and self storage is one of the asset classes where that capital is increasingly active. The Grand Rapids MSA benefits from a durable economic base anchored by office furniture manufacturing, Amway's corporate presence, and a healthcare sector centered on Spectrum Health and the Medical Mile. These are not cyclical employers. They generate the kind of stable household formation and suburban population growth that sustains self storage demand across economic cycles, which is precisely what life insurance companies want to underwrite.

The submarkets that draw the most attention from life company capital in this sector are the infill suburban corridors: Kentwood and Cascade along the 28th Street and M-6 corridors, Walker along the US-131 spine, and Grandville where retail and residential density supports unit absorption. Downtown Grand Rapids and the Medical Mile are viable for climate-controlled urban product, particularly facilities serving the growing multifamily population in those neighborhoods. Life companies are far less interested in rural or exurban locations in Caledonia or outer Sparta, where population density cannot support the occupancy stability their underwriting requires. The I-196 industrial corridor also generates ancillary demand from small business tenants that can complement a suburban facility's tenant mix, which some life company underwriters view favorably.

Michigan's lack of statewide rent control is a meaningful structural positive for life company capital in this state. Life companies underwrite to long hold periods, sometimes 20 years or longer, and regulatory risk to rental rate growth is a real concern in markets like California or Oregon. Michigan removes that uncertainty entirely, which allows underwriters to give full credit to projected rate growth in their cash flow models. For Grand Rapids self storage specifically, that translates to more aggressive proceeds relative to similarly performing assets in rent-controlled jurisdictions.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Grand Rapids Self Storage Deals

Life company underwriting for self storage in this market follows a consistent framework, and sponsors who understand those parameters before approaching a lender will structure better transactions. Stabilized occupancy at or above 85 percent is the baseline expectation, measured on a physical and economic basis over a trailing 12-month period. Life companies are not value-add capital. They are not pricing in a lease-up thesis or giving credit for occupancy that was achieved six months ago after a three-year stabilization. The property needs to demonstrate durable, seasoned performance.

Loan-to-value typically lands between 55 and 65 percent on stabilized Grand Rapids product, with the more favorable end of that range reserved for climate-controlled facilities in high-barrier infill locations. Amortization schedules run 25 to 30 years, which is meaningful for cash-on-cash returns, and term structures range from 10 to 20 years fixed, with 10 and 15 year terms being most common in the current environment. Debt service coverage minimums generally run 1.25x to 1.35x at the underwritten cash flow, and life companies will stress test that coverage against vacancy assumptions that may not match what the property is actually producing at close.

On rate, life company self storage pricing in 2026 is competitive with what the market broadly sees on other institutional product. With the 10-year Treasury trading around 4.3 percent and life company spreads for stabilized, well-located self storage generally running in the 125 to 175 basis point range over that index, all-in rates on quality Grand Rapids deals are landing in a range that compares favorably to CMBS and bank balance sheet alternatives for long-term holds. Climate-controlled, institutional-quality facilities at the tight end of the spread range. Older, non-climate-controlled suburban product will price toward the wider end, assuming the deal qualifies at all. Non-recourse execution is standard, which remains one of life company financing's most durable structural advantages for sponsors with significant net worth at stake.

Life Company Versus Other Permanent Options in Grand Rapids

Life company financing wins on specific deal characteristics, and being honest about where it does and does not compete is part of how we advise sponsors. For self storage, agency financing (Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac) is not a direct competitor because those programs are structured for multifamily. The relevant comparisons are CMBS, bank balance sheet, and debt funds.

Against CMBS, life company execution is generally superior for Grand Rapids self storage when the hold period is 10 years or longer, the loan is non-recourse, and the sponsor values relationship-based underwriting over a process driven by securitization execution windows. CMBS can offer modestly higher proceeds in some cases, but spreads are less predictable, prepayment flexibility is limited, and the securitization process introduces execution risk that long-term holders often find unappealing.

Bank balance sheet debt can offer more flexibility on structure, particularly for shorter holds or transitional situations, but banks in the Grand Rapids market are generally not competing at 10 to 20 year fixed-rate terms with non-recourse carveouts. For a sponsor who wants the certainty of a 15-year fixed rate and clean non-recourse execution, the bank simply is not offering that product.

Debt funds are appropriate for value-add or transitional self storage assets that cannot yet meet life company stabilization thresholds. If a facility in Kentwood is at 75 percent occupancy and needs 18 months to season, a debt fund bridge is the right first step, with a life company refinance as the takeout once stabilization is documented. Life company capital does not compete with bridge lenders on lease-up deals, and we do not try to force that fit.

The life company execution is most compelling for Grand Rapids sponsors who own institutional-quality, stabilized self storage at a meaningful loan size, are planning a long hold, and want fixed-rate non-recourse debt without the execution risk of CMBS or the term limitations of bank financing. When those conditions are met, the life company execution is typically the best available option in this market.
