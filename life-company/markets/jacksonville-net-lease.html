Life Company Net Lease Lending in Jacksonville, FL

Jacksonville has moved well past the secondary market designation that once limited its access to institutional capital. Life insurance companies are actively deploying into this market, drawn by a confluence of fundamentals that align almost perfectly with what long-duration lenders need: a diversifying employment base anchored by Fidelity National Financial and EverBank, a Port of Jacksonville that continues to pull logistics and distribution tenants into the Dames Point and I-95 corridors, and steady in-migration from South Florida and the Northeast that supports sustained retail demand. For net lease specifically, that combination translates into a tenant base that life companies recognize and want exposure to at an institutional level.

The suburban retail submarkets along the Southside and Mandarin corridors have become particularly active for life company net lease capital. Walgreens, Dollar General, AutoZone, and national quick-service restaurant operators have built out substantial footprints in these areas, and life companies underwrite those credit names with confidence. St. Johns County deserves specific attention: as one of the fastest-growing counties in Florida, its retail absorption has been consistent enough to attract life company scrutiny on newly developed NNN sites, provided lease seasoning and tenant co-tenancy satisfy program parameters. The Ponte Vedra and beaches corridor draws similar interest for smaller-format medical and specialty retail.

Florida's regulatory environment strengthens the investment thesis further. The absence of rent control gives life companies clean cash flow assumptions, and the state's landlord-friendly lease enforcement framework reduces the structural risk that makes underwriters cautious in other markets. For net lease in particular, where the entire credit story depends on an uninterrupted payment stream from a single tenant, that legal clarity matters. Life companies funding Jacksonville NNN deals in 2025 and 2026 are pricing that stability into their spread, and borrowers with the right assets and the right tenants are seeing it reflected in execution.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Jacksonville Net Lease Deals

Life company underwriting for Jacksonville net lease assets starts with the tenant, not the real estate. Investment-grade credit is the threshold that unlocks the best executions: DSCR requirements typically run 1.25x to 1.35x on a stressed basis, and LTV is generally constrained to 60 to 65 percent for most credit profiles, with selective stretch to 70 to 75 percent for true investment-grade tenants carrying ten or more years of remaining absolute NNN lease term. Deals that meet both the credit and lease-term tests open the door to full-term interest-only structuring, which materially improves cash-on-cash returns for sponsors during the hold period.

On the rate side, with the 10-year Treasury trading in the 4.25 to 4.40 percent range in early 2026, life company spreads for stabilized Jacksonville NNN assets are generally running 125 to 175 basis points over the 10-year, depending on tenant credit quality, lease term, and property vintage. Investment-grade tenants with long leases push toward the tighter end of that range. The fixed-rate term is typically structured to match or slightly exceed the lease expiration, commonly running 10 to 25 years, which gives the life company a matched duration position and the borrower certainty of execution through lease rollover.

Prepayment structure is yield maintenance or a declining percentage schedule, negotiated at origination and sized to the remaining term. Recourse is non-recourse as a program standard, with standard carve-outs for fraud and environmental. Life companies funding Jacksonville deals are also attentive to property location within the market: properties on primary retail corridors with strong traffic counts and limited near-term competition underwrite better than outlying pads, even with identical tenant credits. Lease language matters equally, and absolute NNN with landlord having zero maintenance or capital expenditure obligations is the baseline requirement.

Life Company versus Other Permanent Options in Jacksonville

Life company execution wins decisively when the deal is stabilized, the tenant is investment-grade, the loan is large enough to justify the fixed costs, and the sponsor intends to hold for the full loan term. For Jacksonville NNN assets in the $5M to $50M range with credit tenants and long leases, life company pricing will generally undercut CMBS by 20 to 50 basis points at current spreads, while offering cleaner non-recourse terms and no securitization complexity.

CMBS is the practical alternative when tenant credit falls below investment-grade, when the loan size is too small for a life company's minimum interest, or when the property profile is more transitional than stabilized. Bank balance sheet lending wins on shorter terms, higher leverage, and recourse tolerance, making it the right tool for value-add acquisitions or lease-up situations that precede a permanent placement. Debt funds fill the gap when leverage requirements exceed 70 percent or when speed of close on an acquisition outweighs cost of capital.

Agency execution through Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac is not applicable to net lease retail, but is highly competitive for multifamily assets in Riverside, Avondale, and St. Johns County where life companies are also active. For those assets, the choice between agency and life company capital depends on loan size, desired prepayment flexibility, and recourse preference. Life companies are generally less flexible on prepayment but competitive on rate for larger, stabilized multifamily with long hold expectations.
