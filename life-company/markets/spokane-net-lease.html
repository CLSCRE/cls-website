Life Company Net Lease Lending in Spokane: Market Context

Spokane has emerged as one of the more active secondary markets for life insurance company net lease capital in the Pacific Northwest, and the reasons are structural rather than cyclical. Eastern Washington's diversified economic base, anchored by Fairchild Air Force Base, a growing healthcare and university sector, and sustained in-migration from higher-cost California markets, has produced the kind of stable retail demand that life companies underwrite against. When a Dollar General or AutoZone signs a 15-year absolute NNN lease in Spokane Valley, the credit underwriting looks nearly identical to the same transaction in Phoenix or Denver, and life company capital prices it accordingly.

The submarkets generating the most life company interest for net lease product are concentrated along the I-90 corridor and the established suburban retail nodes. Spokane Valley and Liberty Lake have absorbed significant NNN development over the past decade, and the tenant mix there, including national drug store chains, quick-service restaurants, auto parts retailers, and dollar store concepts, aligns well with the investment-grade credit profiles life companies require. Airway Heights, immediately adjacent to Fairchild Air Force Base, has also attracted attention for mission-critical retail serving a captive military population, a demand driver life company underwriters view favorably. South Hill remains the strongest submarket for grocery-anchored and shadow-anchored NNN retail given its demographics and household income profile.

Life companies are selective in Spokane, as they are in every secondary market, but selective does not mean absent. The distinction matters. Life company capital in Spokane is available and competitively priced for the right asset, specifically an investment-grade tenant, long remaining lease term, and a property that would perform through an economic downturn. Sponsors bringing stabilized NNN assets in the submarkets described above should expect genuine life company interest. Those with shorter lease terms, local or regional credit tenants, or properties with deferred maintenance will find the life company window narrower and should plan accordingly.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Spokane Net Lease Deals

Life company underwriting for Spokane net lease assets follows a consistent framework, and understanding that framework before approaching the market saves significant time. Loan-to-value expectations typically land in the 60 to 70 percent range, with transactions secured by investment-grade tenants on long absolute NNN leases occasionally reaching 75 percent. Life companies are not leverage lenders. Their pricing advantage is concentrated in the 55 to 65 percent LTV zone, and sponsors who need more proceeds will find the economics of a life company execution less compelling at higher leverage points.

Fixed-rate terms of 10 to 25 years are standard, and the strongest executions are those where the loan term is structured to closely match the remaining lease term. A 15-year lease with 12 years remaining supports a 10-year fixed term cleanly; the same lease with 8 years remaining creates residual lease risk that life company underwriters will price conservatively or decline. In the current rate environment, with the 10-year Treasury hovering near 4.3 percent, life company spreads for investment-grade NNN tenants in Spokane are running in the 125 to 175 basis point range over comparable Treasuries. That pricing reflects the asset quality premium life companies extend to stabilized, credit-backed cash flows and remains among the tightest spread execution available for permanent commercial mortgage financing.

Debt service coverage requirements typically fall in the 1.25x to 1.35x range on actual NOI, though full-term interest-only structures are common for investment-grade tenants with long leases, which materially changes the DSCR calculation at origination. Non-recourse is standard, not a negotiated concession. Yield maintenance or a structured declining prepayment schedule is typical, and sponsors who anticipate an early exit should model that cost carefully before committing to life company execution. The program is designed for long-hold capital, and the prepayment mechanics reflect that intent.

Typical Spokane Life Company Net Lease Deal Profile

The Spokane net lease transactions that close most efficiently through our direct correspondent program share a recognizable profile. Loan sizes range from $2 million to $20 million for single-tenant assets, with portfolio executions scaling higher. The strongest deals involve properties built after 2010 with investment-grade tenants, 10 or more years of absolute NNN lease term remaining, and no deferred maintenance or near-term capital needs. Walgreens, Dollar General, AutoZone, Starbucks, and fast food concepts with franchise guarantees from investment-grade parent companies represent the tenant mix that generates the most competitive pricing.

Sponsor profiles vary, but life companies generally favor experienced owners with clean credit histories and simple ownership structures. Institutional sponsors, family offices, and established private investors with multi-property net lease portfolios are common borrowers. First-time net lease investors can qualify, but the underwriting scrutiny on sponsor financials is higher. Timeline from a signed letter of intent to closing typically runs 45 to 75 days in our direct correspondent program, compared to 75 to 120 days or more when a deal is submitted through an intermediary to a life company's home office. For acquisitions with fixed closing dates, that gap can be decisive.

Life Company versus Other Permanent Options in Spokane

For Spokane net lease assets, life company execution is most competitive when the hold period is long, the tenant is investment-grade, the lease term is extended, and the sponsor values rate certainty over maximum leverage. In those conditions, no other permanent capital source consistently matches life company pricing. CMBS can be competitive on rate for larger loans, but the servicing structure, defeasance requirements, and lack of relationship flexibility make it a second choice for sponsors who manage their assets actively. Bank balance sheet lending offers more structural flexibility but rarely competes on rate for longer fixed terms, and most community and regional banks in Spokane are not offering 15-year fixed-rate non-recourse money on net lease assets.

Debt funds are not the right tool for stabilized net lease financing. They price to risk and leverage, and a 65 percent LTV Dollar General in Spokane Valley does not need the risk premium a debt fund charges. Agency financing through Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac is not applicable to single-tenant net lease retail. For value-add assets, properties with near-term lease rollover, or sponsors needing 75 percent or higher LTV, life company execution is not the right starting point, and we will tell you that directly rather than force an execution that does not fit.

The life company program earns its position in the capital stack when the asset and the business plan align: stabilized, long-term, credit-backed, and held for income. In Spokane, that describes a meaningful and growing segment of the investment market.
