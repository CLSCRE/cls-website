Life Company Office Lending in Detroit: What the Market Actually Looks Like

Detroit's life company lending market has historically concentrated its appetite on industrial, logistics, and suburban multifamily assets, and that pattern has intensified since 2020. The EV manufacturing transition reshaping Metro Detroit through Ford, GM, and Stellantis plant investments has attracted significant life company interest in EV supply chain industrial and logistics assets across Macomb, Oakland, and Monroe Counties. For office product, the picture is more selective, but that selectivity is not a closed door. It is a qualification filter, and sponsors who understand what life companies are actually funding today can still access some of the most competitive long-term fixed-rate capital in the market.

Within the Detroit MSA, life company office interest is concentrated in specific suburban submarkets where tenancy quality, lease term, and asset vintage support the underwriting discipline these lenders require. Troy, Auburn Hills, and Birmingham represent the highest-probability corridors for life company office placement. These submarkets have demonstrated relative occupancy stability, attract regional and national credit tenants in the professional services, healthcare administration, and financial services sectors, and carry the physical characteristics that life companies prefer: suburban campus formats, structured parking, recent capital improvements, and proximity to residential density. Medical office assets in the suburban Detroit market are an increasingly active subset of this conversation, as life companies view healthcare tenancy as durable and structurally defensive against remote-work pressure.

Urban core Detroit office remains fundable for the right sponsorship, but the underwriting bar is higher and the pool of willing life company capital is narrower. Sponsors with proven Detroit track records, existing life company relationships, and assets anchored by long-term government or institutional tenants are the most likely candidates. Speculative or heavily co-working-dependent office product should be directed elsewhere. The honest assessment is that life company office capital in this market rewards certainty of cash flow above almost every other variable, and Detroit's suburban submarkets are where that certainty is most demonstrable today.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Detroit Office Deals

Life company underwriting for office in 2026 reflects the sustained caution that entered the sector post-pandemic and has not fully receded. Loan-to-value expectations have tightened to the 50 to 60 percent range for most product, with the most aggressive pricing reserved for structures at or below 55 percent LTV. Sponsors who arrive with equity-heavy capital stacks and genuine long-term hold intentions are best positioned. Under-leveraged structures command meaningfully better spreads, and in a market where the 10-year Treasury is trading in the mid-4 percent range, life company office spreads of 125 to 175 basis points over benchmark remain competitive against virtually every alternative for stabilized, creditworthy product.

Minimum debt service coverage expectations have also tightened. Life companies underwriting Detroit office today are generally looking for DSCR of 1.30x or better on in-place cash flows, with limited credit given to mark-to-market assumptions or lease-up projections. Weighted average lease term is a critical variable: assets with a WALT of seven years or greater, particularly where anchor tenants have renewal options in place, clear the threshold with more confidence. Multi-tenanted suburban office with no single tenant exceeding 35 to 40 percent of gross leasable area and no near-term rollover concentration is structurally preferred over single-tenant assets where credit is not institutional grade.

Loan terms run 10 to 15 years fixed-rate for stabilized office, with amortization schedules of 25 to 30 years. Non-recourse execution is standard with carve-outs that have broadened somewhat in the current underwriting environment. Yield maintenance is the prepayment standard, which means these loans are structured for sponsors who intend to hold, not sponsors seeking optionality. That alignment between lender structure and borrower strategy is one of the defining features of life company capital and a primary reason it wins for long-term holders of quality assets.

Typical Detroit Life Company Office Deal Profile

A realistic life company office deal in Metro Detroit in 2026 looks something like this: a suburban Class A or strong Class B office asset in Troy, Auburn Hills, or Birmingham, built between 1995 and 2015 with recent capital improvements completed, carrying occupancy of 88 percent or better with a WALT of eight or more years. Loan size falls between $8 million and $35 million. The sponsor is an experienced commercial real estate operator with a demonstrated track record in Michigan or a comparable Midwest market, institutional equity behind the capital stack, and a genuine long-term hold strategy. Medical office assets in suburban Oakland County are particularly strong candidates given healthcare tenancy profiles.

Timeline from executed LOI to closing through our direct correspondent process typically runs 45 to 75 days for qualified deals. Sponsors who have experienced the indirect brokered process should understand that 75 to 120-plus day timelines are common when a submission has to travel through a broker to a home office and back. Our direct process eliminates that lag at the critical pre-commitment and commitment stages, which compresses total deal execution significantly.

Life Company versus Other Permanent Options in Detroit Office

Agency financing through Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac is not applicable to office product and belongs in a different conversation for Detroit multifamily. CMBS remains available for office but has generally widened its spreads relative to life company execution for creditworthy stabilized assets, and CMBS servicing complexity and defeasance costs reduce its appeal for long-term holders. Bank balance sheet lending in Michigan is active for relationship borrowers, but bank appetite for office has retrenched meaningfully since 2023, and terms typically run 5 to 7 years with recourse requirements that life company execution eliminates.

Debt funds fill an important role for Detroit office sponsors with value-add business plans, shorter hold periods, higher leverage requirements, or assets that do not yet meet life company occupancy and lease term thresholds. When a deal needs 70 percent or higher LTV, a 3-year bridge period, or flexibility around lease-up projections, a debt fund is the right conversation, not a life company. Life company execution wins decisively for stabilized, creditworthy assets where sponsors value long-term fixed-rate certainty, non-recourse structure, and the lowest all-in cost of capital available in the permanent lending market.
