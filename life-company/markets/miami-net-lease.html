Life Company Net Lease Lending in Miami, FL

Miami has undergone a fundamental repositioning in the eyes of institutional capital over the past several years, and life insurance companies have taken notice. The COVID-era migration of financial services firms, private equity platforms, and technology companies from the Northeast and California into Brickell, Coral Gables, and the broader South Florida corridor has elevated Miami from a secondary market to a primary allocation target for long-duration institutional capital. For life companies underwriting net lease assets, this shift matters because tenant credit quality and trade area economics are the foundation of any underwriting thesis. Miami's expanding corporate base, rising household incomes, and continued population inflow have strengthened the trade area fundamentals that support credit-tenant retail across the metro.

Florida's structural advantages reinforce life company conviction. The state's no-income-tax environment attracts the high-income households that drive spending at investment-grade retail tenants. Florida state law preempts local rent control, giving life company underwriters a clean, predictable regulatory picture that many gateway markets can no longer offer. Strong collateral values are further supported by Miami's international capital base, where foreign buyers and global institutional investors create durable demand for well-located commercial real estate. For net lease assets specifically, these factors translate into tenant retention, rent escalation, and residual value assumptions that survive even conservative long-term stress scenarios.

Life companies are actively deploying capital on credit-tenant NNN retail across Miami's key commercial corridors, with particular interest in properties anchored by investment-grade names in Coral Gables, Doral, Airport West, and the suburban nodes surrounding Hialeah and Kendall. Properties along high-traffic arterials with strong ingress and visibility, long absolute NNN leases, and tenants with demonstrated South Florida performance metrics are at the top of the allocation list. What life companies are not chasing is marginal credit, short lease term, or locations where trade area demographics have not yet caught up with the development narrative.

What Life Companies Are Looking For in Miami Net Lease Deals

The underwriting profile for life company net lease capital in Miami is deliberate and consistent. Loan-to-value typically lands between 55 and 65 percent on standard transactions, with selected investment-grade tenants carrying long remaining lease terms sometimes qualifying up to 70 to 75 percent. Life companies are pricing risk at the asset level, and a 30-year Walgreens dark value versus a Dollar General with 12 years of term remaining generates meaningfully different advance rates. Debt service coverage requirements are generally in the 1.25x to 1.35x range on a stressed basis, with interest-only structures available and common on true investment-grade credit, which keeps coverage ratios strong even at conservative cap rate assumptions.

In the current rate environment heading into 2026, the 10-year Treasury hovering around 4.30 percent establishes the base for life company pricing. Spread to Treasury on stabilized, credit-tenant NNN assets in Miami runs approximately 125 to 175 basis points for well-structured transactions, placing all-in rates in a range that remains meaningfully competitive with CMBS and bank balance sheet options for the right asset. Fixed-rate terms from 10 to 25 years are available, with most life companies preferring to match the loan term to the remaining lease term on the primary tenant. Non-recourse structure is standard. Yield maintenance or a structured declining prepayment schedule is typical, so sponsors planning on long holds benefit from certainty of term and rate without worrying about a balloon forcing a refinance into an unfavorable environment.

Tenant profile and lease structure are the primary underwriting filters. Investment-grade tenants operating under absolute NNN leases with 10 or more years of remaining term are the target. Walgreens, AutoZone, Dollar General, Starbucks, and similarly rated credits consistently price at the tighter end of the spread range. Operators whose leases contain significant landlord obligations, co-tenancy provisions, or kick-out clauses require additional structure and may widen pricing or reduce proceeds. Miami-specific underwriting also pays close attention to hurricane and flood exposure, with properties in higher-risk zones requiring additional insurance covenant review and sometimes influencing net proceeds.

Typical Miami Life Company Net Lease Deal Profile

A representative life company net lease transaction in Miami looks something like this: a single-tenant retail property in Coral Gables, Doral, or Airport West occupied by an investment-grade credit on a 15-year absolute NNN lease with approximately 12 years of remaining term. The property was built within the last 10 to 20 years, is well-maintained, and sits on a high-traffic commercial corridor with strong demographics within a three-mile ring. Loan size falls between $5 million and $20 million. The borrower is an experienced net lease investor or institutional owner with a clean balance sheet, minimal recourse exposure on other debt, and a stated hold strategy aligned with the remaining lease term.

From signed application to closing, direct correspondent transactions on this profile typically close in 45 to 75 days. There are no surprises at the commitment stage because the underwriting conversation has been ongoing since the initial sizing call. Life Company versus Other Permanent Options in Miami

Life company financing is not the right solution for every net lease deal in Miami, and being direct about that is part of how sophisticated capital markets advisors earn trust. For value-add net lease acquisitions where near-term lease rollover is a variable, a short-term bridge from a debt fund or bank balance sheet is the correct first step. Life companies do not lend into uncertainty. For sponsors who need leverage above 70 percent, CMBS or agency execution on eligible assets will produce higher loan proceeds, though at the cost of non-recourse carve-out exposure and less flexibility on prepayment. For net lease assets under $2 million, bank balance sheet or credit union capital is typically more efficient than pursuing life company execution.

Where life company financing wins decisively is on long-hold, stabilized, credit-tenant assets where rate certainty, full-term interest-only, and non-recourse structure create lasting value. A 20-year fixed rate on a Walgreens or AutoZone with matching lease term eliminates refinance risk entirely and produces predictable cash-on-cash returns that compound over a hold period. CMBS can offer non-recourse and competitive pricing but carries servicer inflexibility, defeasance mechanics that are expensive to unwind, and a secondary market that can price inefficiently during volatility. Bank balance sheet debt is typically limited to 5 to 10 year terms and resets at maturity. For the right asset, life company capital at tight spreads over a long fixed term is simply the most efficient permanent structure available in Miami's net lease market.
