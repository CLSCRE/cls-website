What Makes a Direct Correspondent Different

Most commercial mortgage brokers who offer life company financing are placing your deal with a life insurance company's home office through an intermediary channel. The underwriting decision, approval timeline, and terms are set by someone you never speak to.

Commercial Lending Solutions holds a direct correspondent lender relationship, which means we underwrite these loans ourselves, issue commitments in our name, and control the approval process. Borrowers get a faster timeline, a direct relationship with the decision-maker, and execution certainty that the brokered channel cannot provide.

This applies specifically to the life insurance company permanent loan program. For other products (bridge loans, CMBS, construction), we operate as a commercial mortgage broker with relationships across more than 1,000 lenders.

Why Life Company Permanent Capital

Lowest Long-Term Rates

For stabilized CRE, life company permanent financing typically offers the most competitive long-term fixed rates in the market. Life companies are liability-driven investors -- they want to match long-term assets with long-term liabilities, which is why they price inside other lender types.

10 to 25 Year Fixed Terms

Life companies offer true long-term fixed-rate certainty that banks, debt funds, and even CMBS cannot match. For sponsors with a long-hold thesis, fixing your cost of capital for 15 or 20 years is a structural advantage unavailable from other sources.

Non-Recourse Standard

All life company loans through our correspondent program are non-recourse with standard bad-boy carve-outs. No personal guarantees. Clean exit optionality for ownership groups and institutional sponsors.

Interest-Only Available

Full-term interest-only periods are available for qualified deals -- primarily credit-tenant NNN assets and premier multifamily with strong sponsorship. IO periods improve current cash yield and are a meaningful advantage for distributions-focused ownership structures.

No Securitization Risk

Unlike CMBS loans, life company correspondent loans are held in portfolio. There is no securitization process, no pooling, and no CMBS servicing complexity. The relationship stays with the originating correspondent through the loan term.

Direct Decision-Maker

As the direct correspondent lender, Commercial Lending Solutions underwrites and commits without a third-party approval step. Sponsors deal directly with the decision-maker, not an intermediary who has to check with someone else before responding.
