New York Life lending, clarified.

Does New York Life lend through brokers?

Yes. New York Life Real Estate Investors works with commercial mortgage brokers. CLS CRE places loans with New York Life through direct open-market submission and an active relationship in our lender database. The desk evaluates the asset, sponsorship, and market, then quotes structure and spread.

What loan sizes does New York Life offer?

$25M–$300M+ (portfolios higher). Spreads are set deal-by-deal over Treasury yields; the ranges shown reflect recently executed transactions and current desk guidance.

How do I qualify for a New York Life commercial mortgage?

Stabilized, documented NOI; institutional-quality asset in a proven market; conservative leverage with 1.25x+ DSCR; and a sponsor group with net worth and liquidity. Submit through CLS CRE and we underwrite the deal against the desk's current appetite before it goes anywhere.

How fast can a New York Life loan close?

Typically 45 to 75 days from executed term sheet on correspondent and direct executions, depending on third-party report turnaround and title. CLS manages appraisal, environmental, and property condition through Symetra-style approved vendor lists.
