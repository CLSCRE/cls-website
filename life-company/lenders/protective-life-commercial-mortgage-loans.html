Protective Life lending, clarified.

Does Protective lend through brokers?

Yes. Protective Life — Commercial Mortgages works with commercial mortgage brokers. CLS CRE places loans with Protective through direct open-market submission and an active relationship in our lender database. The desk evaluates the asset, sponsorship, and market, then quotes structure and spread.

What loan sizes does Protective offer?

$3M–$50M per asset. Spreads are set deal-by-deal over Treasury yields; the ranges shown reflect recently executed transactions and current desk guidance.

How do I qualify for a Protective commercial mortgage?

Stabilized, documented NOI; institutional-quality asset in a proven market; conservative leverage with 1.25x+ DSCR; and a sponsor group with net worth and liquidity. Submit through CLS CRE and we underwrite the deal against the desk's current appetite before it goes anywhere.

How fast can a Protective loan close?

Typically 45 to 75 days from executed term sheet on correspondent and direct executions, depending on third-party report turnaround and title. CLS manages appraisal, environmental, and property condition through Symetra-style approved vendor lists.
