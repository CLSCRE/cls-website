How It Works

  • You introduce us to your client — by phone, email, or warm intro. We take it from there.
  • We handle the entire financing process: lender selection, term sheet negotiation, underwriting, closing.
  • Your client relationship stays yours. We never solicit your client for other services.
  • When the loan closes, you receive a referral fee — paid at close, documented in writing upfront.
  • We confirm your referral in writing before any contact with your client.

What We Finance
