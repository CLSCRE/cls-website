A 1031 exchange (named for IRC Section 1031) allows investors to defer capital gains taxes on the sale of investment property by rolling the proceeds into a "like-kind" replacement property. A Qualified Intermediary (QI) holds the sale proceeds and disburses them at the closing of the replacement property. The financing works like a normal purchase loan — you bring equity from the QI, and we arrange debt from a commercial lender. The total must equal or exceed the relinquished property's sale price to defer all taxes.
