Case Study

$20M Community Retail Center in Eagle Rock, Los Angeles, CA

By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

A prominent family-owned retail development company approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking permanent financing for their crown jewel property: a 20 million dollar grocery-anchored community retail center located in the heart of Eagle Rock, Los Angeles. This institutional-quality asset featured 15 inline tenants complementing the grocery anchor, maintaining an impressive 94% occupancy rate that reflected both the property's prime location and the ownership's three decades of expert management.

The borrower's primary objective extended beyond simple refinancing. Having owned the property for over 30 years, they had witnessed remarkable appreciation as Eagle Rock transformed from an emerging neighborhood into one of Los Angeles' most desirable retail corridors. The property had appreciated nearly four times its original value, creating substantial equity that the family wanted to unlock for additional investment opportunities while maintaining ownership of their flagship asset.

The transaction required a sophisticated permanent loan structure that could accommodate the borrower's cash-out needs while providing long-term stability. The family sought to extract 12 million dollars in cash proceeds, increasing their loan amount from 8 million to 20 million dollars, representing the full market value of the property.

The Challenge

Several complex factors made this transaction particularly challenging for traditional lending sources. The substantial cash-out component represented a significant increase in leverage, requiring a lender comfortable with the borrower's business plan and the property's long-term prospects. Many institutional lenders remained cautious about retail properties following recent market volatility, particularly for transactions involving substantial cash extraction.

The borrower required specific loan terms that aligned with their investment strategy. They needed a long-term fixed rate to provide payment certainty and protect against interest rate fluctuations. Additionally, non-recourse financing was essential to limit personal liability while maintaining their ability to pursue other investment opportunities with the extracted capital.

Timing presented another critical challenge. The family had identified several time-sensitive investment opportunities that required quick access to the refinancing proceeds. This necessitated a lender capable of moving efficiently through underwriting and closing processes without compromising on loan terms or structure.

The retail sector's recent headwinds also complicated the financing landscape. Despite the property's strong grocery anchor and consistent occupancy, many lenders had tightened their retail lending criteria, making it difficult to find institutions willing to provide the loan amount and terms the borrower required.

The Solution

Commercial Lending Solutions leveraged its extensive network of institutional capital sources to identify a premier life insurance company perfectly suited for this transaction. Life companies typically offer the most competitive terms for high-quality retail properties with strong sponsorship, making them ideal for this borrower's requirements.

The financing solution featured a 20 million dollar permanent loan structured at 60% loan-to-value, providing the conservative leverage ratio preferred by institutional lenders while meeting the borrower's cash-out objectives. The life company appreciated the property's grocery-anchored tenant mix, recognizing that essential retail uses provided stability even during challenging retail market conditions.

A 10-year fixed rate structure addressed the borrower's desire for long-term payment certainty while aligning with the life company's investment timeline. This fixed rate was priced competitively due to the property's quality, the borrower's experience, and the conservative loan-to-value ratio. The non-recourse structure limited the borrower's personal liability while meeting the life company's underwriting standards.

The lender's streamlined underwriting process enabled rapid progression through due diligence and documentation. Commercial Lending Solutions coordinated closely with all parties to ensure efficient information flow and timely resolution of underwriting requirements, meeting the borrower's aggressive timeline for accessing the refinancing proceeds.

The Outcome

The transaction closed successfully within the borrower's required timeframe, providing 12 million dollars in cash proceeds that enabled the family to pursue their identified investment opportunities while retaining ownership of their premier retail asset. The 10-year fixed rate loan provided payment certainty and protection against interest rate volatility over the loan term.

The conservative 60% loan-to-value ratio satisfied the life company's risk parameters while maximizing the borrower's cash extraction. The non-recourse structure preserved the family's financial flexibility for future investments without personal liability concerns related to the retail property.

This transaction demonstrated Commercial Lending Solutions' ability to navigate complex retail financing scenarios and deliver institutional capital for sophisticated borrowers. The successful execution reinforced the company's reputation for structuring creative solutions that meet borrower objectives while satisfying conservative institutional lender requirements.

The borrower gained access to competitively priced permanent financing that supported their broader investment strategy while maintaining ownership of their trophy asset in one of Los Angeles' premier retail markets.
