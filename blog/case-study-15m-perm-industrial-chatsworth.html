Case Study

$15M Multi-Tenant Industrial in Chatsworth, Los Angeles, CA

Trevor Damyan Founder & Principal Broker
CA DRE #02244836

Former CBRE and Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation. $1B+ in closed commercial real estate financing across bridge, construction, permanent, and structured debt.

April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

A sophisticated real estate investor approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking permanent financing for a premium multi-tenant industrial property in Chatsworth, Los Angeles. The 100,000 square foot facility housed eight tenants across diverse industries, maintaining an impressive 96% occupancy rate. The borrower required $15 million in permanent financing to refinance existing debt and optimize their capital structure.

The property represented a strong cash-flowing asset in one of Los Angeles County's most sought-after industrial submarkets. Chatsworth's strategic location provides exceptional access to major transportation corridors while offering competitive lease rates compared to coastal markets. The facility's tenant mix included established businesses with varying lease terms and industry classifications, creating a diversified income stream.

The borrower sought non-recourse financing with competitive rates and flexible terms that would support their long-term investment strategy. Given the property's performance and location, they expected to achieve institutional-quality financing despite the complexity of the tenant composition.

The Challenge

The primary challenge emerged from the tenant concentration risk within the property. One anchor tenant occupied approximately 35% of the total square footage and contributed a proportional amount of the property's gross rental income. This tenant had only two years remaining on their lease term, creating significant rollover risk that concerned potential lenders.

Traditional lenders viewed the upcoming lease expiration as a potential cash flow disruption that could impact debt service coverage. The concentration risk amplified concerns about the property's ability to maintain current income levels if the anchor tenant chose not to renew or negotiated significantly reduced rental rates.

Additionally, the borrower required terms that many conventional lenders found challenging to accommodate. They specifically needed non-recourse financing without personal guarantees, which limited the pool of available capital sources. The industrial property type, while stable, required lenders with specific expertise in evaluating multi-tenant warehouse and distribution facilities.

Market conditions added another layer of complexity, as rising interest rates had tightened lending standards across commercial real estate. Lenders were scrutinizing tenant quality, lease terms, and property fundamentals more carefully than in previous years.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan leveraged his extensive network of institutional capital sources to identify a life insurance company with deep experience in multi-tenant industrial properties. This lender understood the Chatsworth market dynamics and had previously financed similar assets with comparable tenant profiles.

The life company conducted comprehensive due diligence that extended beyond traditional underwriting metrics. They performed a detailed 15-year occupancy analysis, examining historical tenant retention, market absorption rates, and comparable property performance in the submarket. This analysis demonstrated the property's consistent leasing velocity and the borrower's proven asset management capabilities.

To address the anchor tenant's lease expiration, the life company evaluated the borrower's track record of tenant retention and lease renewals across their portfolio. They also assessed the significant tenant improvement investment of over $500,000 that the anchor tenant had made in their space, which indicated a higher likelihood of lease renewal given the specialized nature of their improvements.

The lender structured the financing at 65% loan-to-value, providing conservative leverage while maximizing proceeds for the borrower. They incorporated a cash sweep provision that would capture excess cash flow above debt service requirements, providing additional security while allowing the borrower to benefit from property performance improvements.

The Outcome

Commercial Lending Solutions successfully secured $15 million in permanent financing for the multi-tenant industrial property. The life insurance company provided a 10-year fixed-rate loan with non-recourse terms, meeting all of the borrower's requirements.

The 65% loan-to-value ratio provided conservative leverage while generating sufficient proceeds to accomplish the borrower's refinancing objectives. The fixed-rate structure protected against interest rate volatility over the loan term, while the non-recourse provision eliminated personal liability for the borrower.

The cash sweep provision created a balanced structure that addressed the lender's concerns about tenant concentration while providing the borrower with upside participation if property performance exceeded projections. This mechanism would automatically reduce if the anchor tenant renewed their lease or if replacement tenants maintained comparable rental rates.

The transaction closed within the projected timeframe despite the complex underwriting requirements. The life company's sophisticated analysis of the tenant improvements and occupancy history proved instrumental in achieving approval at favorable terms.

This financing solution positioned the borrower to maintain their investment strategy while providing the lender with appropriate risk-adjusted returns. The successful completion demonstrated how experienced commercial mortgage professionals can navigate challenging tenant concentration issues through proper lender selection and creative structuring approaches.
