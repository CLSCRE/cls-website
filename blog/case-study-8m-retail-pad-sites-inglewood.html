Case Study

$8,000,000 Net Lease Retail Pad Sites in Inglewood, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan · April 03, 2026 · Permanent Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions recently closed an $8,000,000 permanent financing package for the acquisition of three freestanding retail pad sites in Inglewood, California. The portfolio consists of newly constructed quick-service restaurant and coffee shop locations positioned strategically near the SoFi Stadium and Intuit Dome development corridor.

The properties feature two national quick-service restaurant tenants and one prominent coffee chain, all operating under net lease structures with initial terms exceeding 10 years. The borrower, a private investment group with significant retail experience, sought acquisition financing to capitalize on the area's rapid transformation from secondary market status to an emerging institutional-grade location.

Given the tenant quality and lease structures, the transaction appeared straightforward on the surface. However, the Inglewood location presented unique underwriting challenges that required careful navigation of lender perceptions versus market reality.

The Challenge

The primary obstacle centered on market perception. Despite the massive infrastructure investment surrounding SoFi Stadium and the upcoming Intuit Dome, many institutional lenders continued to underwrite Inglewood properties using historical market data and demographic profiles that no longer reflected current conditions.

Initial lender feedback revealed significant pricing disparities. Several national banks and life companies were applying secondary market spreads, treating the location as a transitional area rather than recognizing its trajectory toward primary market status. This approach would have resulted in pricing 75 to 100 basis points above comparable deals in established primary markets.

The timing element added complexity. While the physical transformation was evident, rental growth and transaction velocity were still developing, creating a gap between current market metrics and future potential. Conservative underwriting departments struggled to justify primary market pricing without extensive comparable sales data that simply did not yet exist in sufficient volume.

Additionally, some lenders expressed concern about the rapid gentrification pace, questioning whether the current tenant base and consumer demographics would sustain long-term performance throughout the lease terms.

The Solution

Our approach focused on repositioning the narrative from historical performance to forward-looking fundamentals. We assembled a comprehensive market analysis that included stadium event calendars, planned commercial developments, and demographic shift projections based on new employment centers and transportation improvements.

Rather than relying solely on local comparables, we presented case studies from similar stadium-anchored retail corridors in other major markets, demonstrating consistent rent growth and cap rate compression patterns following major sports venue developments. This comparative analysis helped quantify the expected trajectory for Inglewood properties.

We targeted life insurance companies with longer investment horizons and greater flexibility in emerging market evaluation. After extensive discussions with multiple institutional sources, we identified a national life company whose investment committee had already recognized the Inglewood opportunity and was actively seeking exposure to the market.

The key breakthrough came when we presented traffic count data and consumer spending analysis from the stadium's inaugural season, demonstrating that actual performance was exceeding initial projections. This current data, combined with the tenant credit quality and lease terms, provided the underwriting support necessary for primary market pricing.

The Outcome

We successfully closed the financing at 65% loan-to-value with a 10-year fixed rate structure on a non-recourse basis. The final pricing reflected primary market treatment, saving the borrower approximately 85 basis points compared to initial secondary market quotes. The loan features a 25-year amortization schedule with no prepayment penalties after year three.

The life company's underwriting ultimately supported our thesis regarding Inglewood's market evolution. Their investment committee approved the deal based on the combination of credit tenant quality, lease structure, and location fundamentals, treating it as an emerging primary market opportunity rather than a secondary market risk.

The borrower achieved their acquisition objectives while positioning for significant appreciation as the corridor continues developing. With additional retail and entertainment venues planned for the area, the properties should benefit from continued rent growth and cap rate compression over the loan term.

This transaction demonstrates the importance of matching the right capital source with emerging market opportunities. While some lenders remain anchored to historical market perceptions, others recognize value in evolving locations with strong fundamental drivers. Success often depends on presenting the appropriate narrative supported by forward-looking analysis rather than relying on backward-looking market data.

The deal also highlights how major infrastructure investments create investment timing opportunities for borrowers willing to move ahead of institutional consensus, provided they can access capital sources with similar market vision.
