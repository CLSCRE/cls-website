Quick Start Checklist

Execute in one session -- estimated 2 to 3 hours

  1. Before you open Google Ads: Pull up this page on one monitor or tab. Have Google Ads Editor open on the other.
  2. Account-level negatives first: Add the negative keyword list in the Account-Level Negatives section below. Do this before creating any campaigns so they propagate automatically.
  3. Create Campaign 1 (Expert Witness): Name, $15/day budget, Max Clicks bidding, national geo, business-hours ad schedule, 4 ad groups, keywords, RSAs, and final URLs per the campaign block below.
  4. Create Campaign 2 (Bridge / Value-Add): $20/day, Max Clicks to start (switch to Target CPA after 30 conversions), national geo, 4 ad groups.
  5. Create Campaign 3 (Construction): $15/day, Max Clicks, national geo, 4 ad groups.
  6. Create Campaign 4 (Professional Referral): $10/day, Max Clicks, national geo, 4 ad groups with respective landing page URLs.
  7. Audiences: Add all in-market and custom intent audiences listed in the Global Settings section as Observation (not Targeting) on each campaign.
  8. Ad schedule: Set Mon to Fri, 8 AM to 6 PM local time on all four campaigns.
  9. Conversion action: Confirm the Contact_1 conversion (loans@clscre.com form submit) is the active conversion goal on all campaigns. Do not add a second conversion action unless you create a dedicated call conversion.
  10. Publish and monitor: Check impressions at 48 hours. If any ad group has zero impressions after 72 hours, widen the keyword list or check policy status.

Budget Pacing and Optimization Notes

Start all four campaigns with Maximize Clicks bidding. Do not switch to Target CPA or Target ROAS until you have at least 30 conversions in a 30-day period per campaign. For Expert Witness (Campaign 1), which will likely generate fewer but higher-value conversions, stay on Maximize Clicks longer. A single engaged expert witness retainer justifies months of ad spend.

Check spend pacing at 7 days. If any campaign exhausts its daily budget before 3 PM, either raise the daily cap or add negative keywords to reduce unqualified clicks first. Do not raise bids until you have confirmed the traffic quality is worth it.

The total new spend is $60/day, which aligns with the $20 to $50 new spend target when combined with the fact that some of this is replacing or supplementing existing bridge and construction campaigns. If the existing bridge campaign was already at $20/day, consolidate it into Campaign 2 rather than running two bridge campaigns simultaneously.

Review search term reports at 14 days. Add any irrelevant queries as negative keywords at the campaign level. Pay particular attention to expert witness ad groups where informational queries ("what is an expert witness," "expert witness training") may slip through despite account-level negatives.
