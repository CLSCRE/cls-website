Case Study

$48M Industrial Distribution Campus in Gateway Cities, Los Angeles, CA

By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

A sophisticated real estate investment group approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking permanent financing for a substantial industrial distribution campus in the Gateway Cities submarket of Los Angeles County. The 400,000 square foot facility represents a prime example of modern logistics infrastructure, strategically positioned to serve the greater Los Angeles metropolitan area's distribution needs.

The property features a single, creditworthy tenant under a 12-year triple net lease structure, providing stable cash flow and minimal landlord responsibilities. Located in one of Southern California's most active industrial corridors, the facility serves as a critical distribution hub connecting the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach to inland markets.

The borrower sought $48 million in permanent financing to refinance existing debt and optimize their capital structure. With the industrial sector experiencing unprecedented demand driven by e-commerce growth and supply chain reconfiguration, the timing was ideal to secure favorable long-term financing terms.

The Challenge

Despite the asset's strong fundamentals, the $48 million loan amount presented unique challenges in the institutional lending market. Many life insurance companies, traditionally the most competitive lenders for stabilized industrial properties, expressed interest but required loan participations due to single-asset concentration limits.

Participation structures often introduce complexity, extended timelines, and potential complications during the loan term. Multiple parties must align on underwriting standards, documentation requirements, and ongoing asset management decisions. This complexity can create uncertainty for borrowers seeking streamlined execution.

Additionally, the borrower prioritized non-recourse financing with flexible prepayment options, further narrowing the pool of potential lenders. The combination of loan size, non-recourse structure, and desired terms required careful lender selection and positioning.

Market conditions also presented timing pressures, as industrial asset values were at historic highs and interest rates showed signs of potential volatility. The borrower needed certainty of execution within a compressed timeline to optimize their financing costs.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan and the Commercial Lending Solutions team leveraged their extensive institutional relationships to identify lenders capable of holding the entire $48 million commitment. Rather than accepting participation structures, the team focused on life companies with larger single-asset capacity and strong appetites for West Coast industrial properties.

The marketing strategy emphasized the asset's location within the supply-constrained Gateway Cities submarket, where industrial vacancy rates remain at historic lows. The presentation highlighted the tenant's strong credit profile, the strategic importance of the facility within their distribution network, and the substantial lease term remaining.

Detailed market analysis demonstrated the property's competitive advantages, including proximity to major transportation infrastructure, modern clear heights exceeding 30 feet, and efficient truck court configuration. The team positioned the asset as institutional-quality logistics real estate in one of the nation's most liquid industrial markets.

The financing package was structured to provide maximum flexibility while meeting the lender's risk parameters. Key terms included interest-only payments for the initial seven years, providing enhanced cash flow for the borrower's business plan.

The Outcome

Commercial Lending Solutions successfully secured $48 million in permanent financing from a single life insurance company, eliminating participation complexity while delivering highly competitive terms. The 10-year fixed-rate structure provides long-term rate certainty, while the 55% loan-to-value ratio reflects conservative leverage appropriate for institutional ownership.

The seven-year interest-only payment structure enhances the borrower's cash-on-cash returns and provides flexibility for future strategic decisions. Non-recourse terms protect the borrower's other assets while maintaining competitive pricing typically associated with full-recourse financing.

The streamlined execution process closed within 45 days of application, allowing the borrower to capitalize on favorable market conditions. The single-lender structure ensures efficient ongoing loan administration and decision-making throughout the 10-year term.

This transaction demonstrates Commercial Lending Solutions' ability to navigate complex institutional requirements while delivering optimal outcomes for borrowers. The successful placement with a single lender at a competitive rate reflects deep market knowledge and strong lender relationships built over years of consistent deal flow.

The borrower achieved their primary objectives of long-term rate certainty, non-recourse structure, and flexible cash flow through the interest-only period. The financing positions them to maximize returns from this institutional-quality industrial asset while maintaining optimal capital structure flexibility.
