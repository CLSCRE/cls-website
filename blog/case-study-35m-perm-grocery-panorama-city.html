Case Study

$35M Grocery-Anchored Shopping Center in Panorama City, Los Angeles, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions recently structured a $35 million permanent financing solution for a 150,000 square foot grocery-anchored shopping center in Panorama City, Los Angeles. The property features a dominant grocery anchor tenant alongside 22 inline retail spaces, maintaining an impressive 93% occupancy rate across the entire center.

Located in a densely populated working-class neighborhood, this shopping center serves as a critical retail hub for the surrounding community. The grocery anchor demonstrates exceptional performance metrics, generating sales per square foot in the top quartile for its market segment. This strong operational performance, combined with the property's strategic location and diverse tenant mix, positioned the asset as an attractive investment opportunity despite its location in what some lenders perceive as a secondary market.

The borrower sought permanent financing to refinance existing debt and optimize their capital structure for long-term ownership. The financing request required a sophisticated approach given the property's location characteristics and the current commercial real estate lending environment.

The Challenge

The primary challenge centered on overcoming lender perceptions of the Panorama City submarket. Many traditional commercial lenders classify this working-class area as secondary or tertiary, automatically applying more conservative underwriting standards that can limit financing options and increase borrowing costs.

Several lenders initially approached the deal with skepticism, focusing primarily on demographic data and area income levels rather than the property's strong operational fundamentals. This narrow perspective led to preliminary offers with higher interest rates, lower loan-to-value ratios, and more restrictive terms than the borrower required.

The property's wider capitalization rate, while reflecting the perceived market risk, actually created stronger debt service coverage ratios than comparable assets in prime locations. However, communicating this advantage to potential lenders required extensive market analysis and performance documentation.

Additionally, the size of the financing request limited the pool of available lenders. The $35 million loan amount exceeded the capacity of many regional banks while falling below the preferred minimum for some institutional lenders, creating a challenging middle-market financing scenario.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan and the Commercial Lending Solutions team developed a comprehensive marketing strategy that repositioned the property's perceived weaknesses as operational strengths. The approach focused on demonstrating how the wider capitalization rate environment translated directly into superior debt service coverage ratios, providing lenders with enhanced security and reduced credit risk.

The team prepared detailed market analysis highlighting the grocery anchor's exceptional sales performance, ranking in the top quartile for sales per square foot within its category. This data proved the location's retail viability and consumer spending power, directly contradicting assumptions about the area's commercial potential.

Recognizing that traditional bank financing might not provide optimal terms, the team targeted CMBS conduits with proven experience in similar markets and asset types. CMBS lenders often demonstrate greater appetite for properties in working-class areas when supported by strong operational metrics and stable cash flows.

The presentation emphasized the property's defensive characteristics, including the necessity-based grocery anchor, diverse tenant mix, and limited local competition. These factors provided compelling evidence of income stability and tenant retention prospects, key considerations for long-term permanent financing.

The Outcome

Commercial Lending Solutions successfully secured $35 million in CMBS financing at 70% loan-to-value ratio, meeting the borrower's leverage requirements while maintaining competitive pricing. The 10-year fixed-rate structure provided long-term rate certainty, with interest-only payments for the initial three years to optimize cash flow during the early loan period.

The financing terms significantly exceeded the borrower's initial expectations, particularly given the challenging market perceptions they had encountered with previous lender discussions. The interest-only period provides valuable cash flow flexibility for property improvements and operational enhancements.

The successful closing demonstrated how proper positioning and targeted lender selection can overcome market perception challenges. By focusing on lenders with demonstrated experience in similar assets and submarkets, the team avoided the bias that had limited previous financing attempts.

The transaction serves as a model for financing grocery-anchored retail properties in working-class markets throughout Los Angeles and similar metropolitan areas. The deal structure and underwriting approach provide a template for future transactions involving operationally strong assets in markets that may be overlooked by mainstream commercial lenders.

This financing solution enables the borrower to maintain long-term ownership of a cash-flowing asset while benefiting from the area's continued demographic growth and retail demand. The successful completion reinforces the value of specialized commercial mortgage brokerage expertise in navigating complex market perceptions and lender requirements.
