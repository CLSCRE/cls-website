$10M Retail Repositioning in Westwood, Los Angeles, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Bridge Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions arranged a $10 million bridge loan for the repositioning of a retail center in Westwood, Los Angeles, positioned strategically near UCLA. The property faced significant challenges with two anchor tenants having recently vacated, leaving the center at just 60% occupancy. The borrower's vision involved transforming the traditional retail space into a modern mixed-use destination focusing on food and beverage concepts alongside fitness facilities to better serve the surrounding university community and residential population. The financing package featured a 65% loan-to-value ratio with a 12% interest rate over an 18-month term. This bridge loan structure provided the borrower with the necessary capital to execute their repositioning strategy while maintaining flexibility during the lease-up period. The private lender recognized the property's strong location fundamentals and the borrower's comprehensive business plan for the transformation.

The Challenge

The primary challenge centered around the property's current vacancy status and the inherent risks associated with retail repositioning in a changing market environment. With two major anchor spaces vacant, the property generated limited cash flow, making traditional bank financing unavailable. Conventional lenders viewed the project as too risky given the speculative nature of the repositioning and the uncertainty surrounding new tenant absorption. The location near UCLA presented both opportunity and complexity. While the university provided a steady customer base, retail preferences among college students and young professionals had shifted significantly toward experiential dining and fitness concepts rather than traditional retail offerings. The borrower needed financing that would bridge the gap between the current state and the projected stabilized occupancy following successful re-tenanting. Market conditions added another layer of complexity, as retail lending had tightened considerably following widespread changes in consumer shopping patterns. Lenders were increasingly cautious about retail properties, particularly those requiring significant repositioning efforts. The borrower needed a financing partner who understood the local market dynamics and could evaluate the deal based on future potential rather than current performance.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan at Commercial Lending Solutions identified a private lender who specialized in retail repositioning projects and understood the unique dynamics of the Westwood market. The solution involved structuring the loan based on the going-in value of the property plus the strength of signed letters of intent from prospective tenants in the food and beverage and fitness sectors. The private lender's underwriting approach focused on the strategic location advantages, including proximity to UCLA, high foot traffic patterns, and strong demographic profiles of the surrounding area. Rather than relying solely on current cash flow, the lender evaluated the borrower's track record in similar repositioning projects and the quality of the signed tenant commitments. The 18-month term provided sufficient runway for the borrower to complete tenant improvements and achieve stabilized occupancy. The loan structure included interest-only payments during the initial phase, preserving capital for repositioning efforts. The 12% interest rate reflected the project's risk profile while remaining competitive for bridge financing in the Los Angeles market. Commercial Lending Solutions coordinated with the lender to streamline the due diligence process, ensuring all parties understood the repositioning timeline and milestones. The financing package included provisions for tenant improvement allowances and leasing commissions, providing the borrower with comprehensive capital to execute their business plan.

The Outcome

The $10 million bridge loan successfully closed within 45 days of initial application, enabling the borrower to move quickly on their repositioning strategy. The efficient closing timeline was crucial given the competitive nature of the Los Angeles retail market and the need to secure quality tenants for the transformation. The private lender's confidence in the project, demonstrated through their willingness to fund based on going-in value plus signed letters of intent, validated the repositioning concept and provided the borrower with credibility in tenant negotiations. This financing approach allowed the property owner to offer competitive tenant improvement packages to attract the desired food and beverage and fitness concepts. The successful financing positioned the property for its transformation from a struggling traditional retail center to a vibrant, experiential destination serving the UCLA community. The loan structure provided the flexibility needed for repositioning while maintaining reasonable leverage levels that supported long-term project viability. Commercial Lending Solutions' ability to identify the right capital source and structure the transaction appropriately demonstrated the value of specialized commercial real estate financing expertise. The deal showcased how creative financing solutions can unlock value in challenging retail properties when paired with strong market knowledge and experienced execution. This transaction exemplified successful bridge lending in the retail sector, where understanding local market dynamics, tenant demand patterns, and repositioning strategies proved essential to achieving a positive outcome for all stakeholders involved in the financing.
