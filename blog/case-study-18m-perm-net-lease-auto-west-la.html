Case Study

$18M Net Lease Auto Dealership in West Los Angeles, CA

Trevor Damyan Founder & Principal Broker
CA DRE #02244836

Former CBRE and Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation. $1B+ in closed commercial real estate financing across bridge, construction, permanent, and structured debt.

April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

A sophisticated real estate investor approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking permanent financing for an $18 million acquisition of a strategically located auto dealership property in West Los Angeles, California. The property featured a nationally recognized automotive brand operating under a 20-year triple net ground lease structure, positioning this as an attractive passive investment opportunity for the buyer.

The dealership property represented a prime example of credit tenant lease financing, with the tenant being backed by one of the major automotive manufacturers in the United States. The investor's acquisition strategy focused on securing stable, long-term cash flow through this institutional-quality net lease asset in one of the most desirable automotive retail markets in Southern California.

The financing requirement called for a permanent loan structure that would accommodate the investor's buy-and-hold investment thesis while providing competitive terms that reflected the high-quality nature of the underlying real estate and tenant creditworthiness.

The Challenge

Auto dealership financing presents unique complexities that many traditional commercial lenders struggle to underwrite effectively. The primary challenge centered on finding a lender with deep understanding of automotive retail real estate and the intricate relationship between dealership operations, manufacturer support programs, and underlying property values.

Many conventional commercial real estate lenders lack the specialized knowledge required to properly evaluate dealership credit quality, particularly the nuances of manufacturer-backed tenant strength and the operational requirements that drive dealership location values. The environmental considerations typical with automotive properties also create additional layers of due diligence complexity.

The West Los Angeles location, while highly desirable, commanded premium real estate values that required a lender comfortable with the market dynamics and long-term viability of automotive retail in high-cost urban markets. Additionally, the investor sought non-recourse financing terms, which further limited the pool of potential lending partners willing to provide competitive execution.

Timing considerations added another layer of complexity, as the investor needed to move quickly to secure the property in a competitive acquisition environment while ensuring proper due diligence and financing certainty.

The Solution

Commercial Lending Solutions leveraged its extensive network of specialty lenders to identify a life insurance company with a dedicated automotive dealership lending program. This lender maintained deep expertise in evaluating manufacturer-backed dealership credits and understood the operational dynamics that drive successful automotive retail locations.

The life company's specialized knowledge of the automotive sector enabled them to properly underwrite the manufacturer support structure and evaluate the tenant's long-term viability within the brand network. Their experience with similar dealership investments across major metropolitan markets provided confidence in the West Los Angeles location's fundamentals.

The lender's automotive dealership program offered terms specifically structured for these property types, including appropriate loan-to-value ratios that reflected their comfort with dealership real estate as collateral. Their underwriting approach incorporated both the real estate fundamentals and the operational aspects of the dealership business model.

Commercial Lending Solutions coordinated the complex due diligence process, working closely with the lender's specialized underwriting team to ensure all automotive-specific requirements were properly addressed while maintaining the transaction timeline required for successful closing.

The Outcome

The transaction closed successfully with highly competitive terms that exceeded the investor's initial expectations. The life insurance company provided $18 million in permanent financing at 60% loan-to-value, reflecting their confidence in both the West Los Angeles market and the manufacturer-backed tenant credit.

The investor secured a 10-year fixed-rate structure that provided long-term payment certainty aligned with their investment holding period. The non-recourse nature of the financing protected the investor's other assets while providing the leverage needed to optimize investment returns on this passive real estate holding.

The financing terms recognized the premium quality of the manufacturer-backed tenant and the strategic value of the West Los Angeles location within the broader automotive retail landscape. The lender's specialized automotive program enabled pricing and structure that reflected the true risk profile of this investment-grade net lease property.

This successful execution demonstrated the critical importance of matching specialized property types with lenders who maintain dedicated expertise and lending programs designed specifically for those asset classes. The investor achieved their acquisition objectives while establishing a lending relationship with a life company committed to the automotive dealership sector for future investment opportunities.

The transaction exemplified how proper lender selection and specialized market knowledge can unlock optimal financing solutions for unique commercial real estate investments, particularly in specialized sectors like automotive retail that require deep industry understanding to underwrite effectively.
