Capital Markets Report: Week of June 8, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, and lender appetite heading into mid-summer 2026.

Rate Environment

Commercial mortgage rates held in a narrow band during the week of June 8–12, 2026. Mixed labor data and cautious Fed language kept Treasury yields range-bound.

Key Themes This Week

  • Long-term fixed-rate seekers saw modest opportunities as Treasuries stayed constructive.
  • Bridge and transitional lending remained more expensive, but debt fund appetite for value-add and repositioning improved.
  • Construction borrowers continue facing compressed feasibility due to SOFR levels + costs + exit caps.
  • Refinance candidates facing 2024–2025 maturities should not wait for dramatically better rates.

Action Items

  • Get rate quotes now on deals inside 90 days of closing.
  • Stress-test at current and slightly higher rates.
  • Engage early on upcoming maturities.
  • Clean up financial packages (rent rolls, T12s) before going to market.
