The Deal That Walked in the Door

We are Commercial Lending Solutions, a commercial mortgage brokerage based in Los Angeles, and this deal brought us a 38-unit apartment community in Alameda, California, in the East Bay, that needed a refinance built around near-term cash flow flexibility. The ownership wanted proceeds sized to the property's value with an interest-only runway before full amortization kicked in, rather than a straight fully amortizing structure from day one.

Getting a bank to combine a competitive fixed-rate period with a meaningful interest-only stretch on a 38-unit property meant finding a lender willing to underwrite the deal's income today while giving ownership room to execute over the next two years. Commercial Lending Solutions specializes in multifamily refinancing and hybrid-rate bank financing for property owners across California.

What Made It Hard

  • Structuring both an interest-only period and a hybrid rate. Layering a 24-month interest-only stretch on top of a 5-year fixed-to-adjustable rate structure meant coordinating two separate features that most lenders don't offer together without pushing pricing higher.
  • A tight commitment timeline. With the original conditional commitment issued August 11, 2021 and an updated commitment following just two days later expiring August 23, 2021, ownership had to move fast through insurance documentation, personal financial statements, and entity resolution updates.
  • Entity and vesting cleanup before funding. The bank required updated meeting minutes and amendments for the borrowing LLC to be fully executed, along with confirmation of how title would be held, adding a documentation step that had to clear before loan documents could be drawn.

How We Ran the Process

Trevor Damyan, a California-licensed real estate broker (CA DRE 02244836), placed the refinance with Umpqua Bank, with the updated commitment letter dated August 13, 2021:

  • $9,500,000 secured by a first trust deed on the Alameda property, with no secondary financing permitted
  • 3.10% fixed for the first five years, then resetting to 6-month LIBOR plus a 2.15% margin, subject to a 3.10% floor and a 9.50% ceiling, with a maximum 1.00% semi-annual adjustment thereafter
  • A 24-month interest-only period, followed by a 30-year amortization schedule over the balance of the 30-year loan term
  • A $0 loan origination fee, alongside a $14,250 loan processing fee
  • A declining prepayment penalty of 4% in year one, 3% in year two, 2% in year three, and 1% in year four
  • A limited personal guaranty from the ownership's principal, alongside signed and dated financial statements and historical operating statements

The refinance closed within the updated commitment's window, giving ownership a competitive fixed rate, a two-year interest-only runway, and a clear path into long-term amortization on their Alameda apartment community.

"Most banks will give you the fixed rate or the interest-only period, not both. Getting the operating history and rent roll in front of them clean and early is what convinced them to underwrite both features together."
, Trevor Damyan, Commercial Lending Solutions

Why We Won This One

Getting the bank comfortable pairing a 24-month interest-only period with a 5-year fixed rate, rather than one or the other, is what delivered the cash-flow flexibility this ownership group needed. Presenting the property's operating history and rent roll clearly upfront let the bank move to a conditional commitment quickly rather than requesting round after round of additional underwriting.

Clearing the entity and vesting cleanup items fast, once the updated commitment's tighter window was set, kept the refinance from slipping past its expiration date and let ownership close on the terms they'd already been quoted rather than risking a repriced extension.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

An interest-only period lowers monthly debt service in the early years of a loan, freeing up cash flow for capital improvements, lease-up, or return to ownership before full amortization begins. This refinance carried a 24-month interest-only period before converting to a 30-year amortization schedule.
A hybrid structure locks in a fixed rate for an initial period, then resets to a floating index plus a margin for the remainder of the term, often with a floor and ceiling limiting how far the rate can move. This loan fixed at 3.10% for the first five years, then reset to 6-month LIBOR plus 2.15%, floored at 3.10% and capped at 9.50%, with a maximum 1.00% semi-annual adjustment thereafter.
A declining prepayment penalty charges a percentage of the outstanding balance that steps down each year, giving the borrower increasing flexibility to refinance or sell as the loan seasons. This loan carried a 4% penalty in year one, stepping down to 3%, 2%, and 1% in years two through four.
A bank may waive its origination fee to win a deal on price while still recovering its underwriting and processing costs through a separate loan processing fee, keeping the headline cost of the loan competitive. This refinance carried a $0 loan fee alongside a $14,250 loan processing fee.
