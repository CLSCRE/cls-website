Case Study

$26,000,000 Cold Storage Facility in Vernon, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan · April 03, 2026 · Permanent Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions recently closed a $26 million permanent financing for a specialized cold storage facility in Vernon, California. The 150,000 square foot refrigerated warehouse serves as a critical node in Los Angeles County's food distribution network, featuring advanced blast freezing capabilities, multiple temperature zones, and direct rail access for efficient cargo handling.

The property operates under a single tenant structure with a major food distribution company occupying the facility on a 10-year lease term. Vernon's strategic location within the industrial corridor east of downtown Los Angeles makes it the epicenter of the region's food logistics infrastructure, with proximity to major transportation arteries including Interstate 710 and multiple rail lines connecting to the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach.

The Challenge

Cold storage facilities present unique valuation and underwriting challenges that eliminate most conventional commercial real estate lenders from consideration. The refrigeration systems, including ammonia-based cooling equipment, blast freezing technology, and temperature control infrastructure, represent millions of dollars in specialized mechanical systems that are integral to the building's functionality and value.

Traditional bank appraisers typically struggle to properly assess cold storage facilities because they lack expertise in valuing the sophisticated refrigeration equipment and fail to understand the premium that specialized industrial users place on these assets. Most lenders view the mechanical systems as depreciating equipment rather than value-add components, resulting in conservative valuations that significantly limit loan proceeds.

The borrower initially approached several regional banks and received financing proposals based on generic warehouse valuations that ignored the cold storage premium. These lenders treated the property as standard industrial space, discounting the specialized infrastructure that commanded higher rents and attracted credit-worthy food industry tenants willing to sign long-term leases.

The Solution

Commercial Lending Solutions identified this as a specialty asset requiring a lender with dedicated cold chain and food logistics expertise. Rather than pursuing conventional bank financing, we targeted institutional lenders with sector-specific knowledge and experience valuing refrigerated warehouse facilities.

We connected the borrower with a national life insurance company that maintains a dedicated food logistics lending team within their commercial real estate division. This lender regularly finances cold storage facilities, distribution centers, and food processing plants, giving them the specialized knowledge necessary to properly underwrite the asset.

The life company engaged an appraiser with cold storage expertise who understood how to value the refrigeration systems as integral building components rather than depreciating equipment. The appraisal properly reflected the facility's specialized nature and the premium that cold storage commands in Vernon's tight industrial market.

We structured the financing as a 10-year fixed-rate permanent loan with a 25-year amortization schedule. The deal closed at 60% loan-to-value based on the higher appraised value that properly accounted for the cold storage infrastructure. The loan was structured as non-recourse financing, providing the borrower with liability protection while maintaining competitive pricing.

The Outcome

The life insurance company's specialized approach resulted in an appraised value significantly higher than what traditional bank appraisers had estimated. This higher valuation enabled loan proceeds that exceeded the borrower's original expectations and provided additional capital for other investments.

The 10-year fixed-rate structure offers the borrower interest rate certainty that matches well with their business planning horizon, while the non-recourse feature provides downside protection. The 25-year amortization schedule maintains reasonable debt service coverage while building equity over time.

Most importantly, the borrower avoided the frustration of working with lenders who fundamentally misunderstood their asset class. The life company's food logistics team appreciated the facility's strategic location within Vernon's food distribution corridor and the stability provided by the long-term tenant relationship with an established food distribution operator.

This transaction demonstrates the importance of matching specialized assets with lenders who have relevant sector expertise. Cold storage facilities require underwriters who understand food distribution logistics, refrigeration technology, and the premium economics of temperature-controlled warehousing. Generic commercial real estate lenders simply lack the knowledge base to properly evaluate these assets, leading to suboptimal financing outcomes for borrowers.

For owners of specialized industrial assets like cold storage facilities, data centers, or manufacturing plants, the key to optimizing financing terms lies in identifying lenders with dedicated expertise in those sectors rather than settling for commodity warehouse financing from conventional sources.
