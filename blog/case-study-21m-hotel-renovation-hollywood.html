Case Study

$21,000,000 Boutique Hotel Renovation in Hollywood, CA

By Trevor Damyan · April 03, 2026 · Bridge Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions recently closed a $21 million bridge loan for the acquisition and comprehensive renovation of an 85-room boutique hotel in Hollywood, California. The property, situated on a high-visibility boulevard in close proximity to major entertainment studios, represents a significant value-add opportunity in one of Los Angeles' most dynamic hospitality submarkets.

The borrower acquired the distressed asset out of receivership at a substantial discount to replacement cost, with plans to execute a complete repositioning from a budget accommodation to an upscale boutique lifestyle brand. The scope of work includes full interior renovation, common area redesign, systems upgrades, and brand repositioning to capture the premium segment of Hollywood's recovering hospitality market.

The Challenge

This transaction presented one of the most complex risk profiles in commercial real estate financing: the intersection of hospitality risk, distressed asset acquisition, construction risk, and market repositioning risk in a single deal structure. The borrower's acquisition of a receivership property added layers of due diligence complexity, while the planned renovation introduced construction timing and cost overrun exposure.

The hospitality sector's inherent operational volatility, combined with the uncertainty of repositioning a budget property into the boutique segment, created additional underwriting challenges. Four bridge lenders declined the transaction, citing the cumulative risk profile and their limited appetite for hospitality construction deals in the current market environment.

Traditional construction lenders viewed the hospitality component as too speculative, while hospitality-focused lenders expressed concerns about the construction and repositioning elements. The receivership acquisition history further complicated title and environmental due diligence, requiring extensive third-party reports and legal documentation.

The Solution

Through our network of specialized debt funds, we identified a hospitality-focused lender with demonstrated expertise in similar repositioning projects throughout the Los Angeles market. This particular debt fund had successfully financed three comparable hotel repositioning transactions in Los Angeles over the previous 24 months and possessed deep institutional knowledge of Hollywood's hospitality market recovery trajectory.

The lender's underwriting team had extensive experience with receivership acquisitions and understood the value creation potential in Hollywood's boutique hotel segment. Their previous transactions included similar budget-to-boutique repositioning projects, providing them with reliable cost and timeline benchmarks for renovation execution.

We structured the financing to address both acquisition and construction needs through a comprehensive bridge loan facility. The deal structure included 70% loan-to-cost on the acquisition price, with 100% of the renovation budget funded through a controlled draw process tied to construction milestones and third-party inspections.

The Outcome

The final loan structure provided $21 million in total financing at SOFR plus 450 basis points with a 30-month term. The interest-only structure during the construction and lease-up periods provides cash flow flexibility while the property undergoes renovation and repositioning.

The controlled draw mechanism ensures renovation funds are deployed efficiently while providing the lender with construction oversight and milestone verification. The 30-month term allows adequate time for renovation completion, brand repositioning, and operational stabilization before permanent refinancing.

Based on comparable boutique hotel transactions in Hollywood and the borrower's detailed renovation and operating plans, the project is projected to generate a 3x equity multiple upon stabilization and permanent refinancing. The borrower plans to refinance into permanent financing once the property achieves 12 months of stabilized operations under the new boutique brand positioning.

This transaction demonstrates the importance of lender specialization in complex, multi-layered deals where traditional financing sources lack the expertise or risk appetite. The successful closing required a lender with deep sector knowledge, relevant transaction experience, and the ability to underwrite the intersection of acquisition, construction, and operational risks in a single financing structure.

The Hollywood hospitality market's continued recovery, combined with limited boutique hotel inventory in prime locations, supports the repositioning strategy and projected returns. The borrower's experienced hospitality management team and proven track record in similar renovations provided additional confidence in execution capability.
