Case Study: $14M Entitled Land Bridge Loan with Cash-Out in Downtown Los Angeles

By Trevor Damyan July 27, 2026
Quick answer: Commercial Lending Solutions arranged a $14,000,000 bridge loan through a private bridge lender for an entitled development site in Downtown Los Angeles, approved for 232 residential condominium units and nine commercial condominium units. The loan refinanced existing debt and provided cash out to fund the completion of the entitlement process, structured as a 12-month bridge at Prime plus 5.75% with a 10.0% floor.

The Deal That Walked in the Door

We are Commercial Lending Solutions, a commercial mortgage brokerage based in Los Angeles, and this bridge financing brought us a developer holding an entitled land site in Downtown Los Angeles, CA with an approved Vesting Tentative Tract Map for a substantial mixed residential-commercial condominium project. Rather than needing construction capital, the ownership needed to refinance its existing debt on the land while pulling additional cash out specifically to fund the remaining costs of completing the entitlement process before the project could move to vertical construction. Commercial Lending Solutions specializes in entitled land bridge loans and construction financing for developers across Los Angeles and nationwide.

Entitled land in Downtown LA carries real value once a project has secured its density and use rights through a Vesting Tentative Tract Map, but that value is illiquid until a lender is willing to underwrite against the entitlement itself rather than an operating income stream, which is exactly the kind of bridge a private capital lender like a private bridge lender is built to provide.

What Made It Hard

  • Sizing a loan against entitlement value, not cash flow. With no operating income on a raw entitled site, the lender's underwriting rested on the strength and durability of the approved Vesting Tentative Tract Map itself, a fundamentally different collateral analysis than a standard income-producing property.
  • Structuring genuine cash-out proceeds within a tight 12-month bridge term. Beyond simply refinancing the existing debt, the facility had to free up real proceeds earmarked for entitlement completion costs, all while the loan itself carried a one-year maturity that left limited runway before repayment or refinance was due.
  • A rate floor above the formula rate for most of the loan's life. With the loan priced at Prime plus 5.75% but floored at 10.0%, the effective rate stayed at the floor unless Prime moved well above its levels at the time, meaning the borrower had to underwrite the deal to the floor rate as the realistic cost of capital.
"Land with no income stream is a hard sell to most lenders, but a private bridge lender understood that an approved Vesting Tentative Tract Map is its own form of collateral value. Structuring the cash out specifically toward entitlement completion, not just as a straight payoff, is what kept the lender's underwriting comfortable."
, Trevor Damyan, Commercial Lending Solutions

How We Ran the Process

Trevor Damyan, a California-licensed real estate broker (CA DRE 02244836), placed the bridge financing with a private bridge lender, a private capital lender, with the signed commitment letter accepted in late 2020:

  • $14,000,000 secured by a first-priority deed of trust on the entitled Downtown LA site, approximately 35,086 square feet of land with an approved Vesting Tentative Tract Map for 232 residential condominium units and nine commercial condominium units totaling roughly 260,000 square feet of floor area
  • A 12-month maturity from the closing date, with all outstanding principal and interest due in full at maturity
  • An interest rate of Prime plus 5.75%, floored at 10.0%, with interest-only monthly payments commencing the first calendar month after disbursement
  • A 1.0 point loan fee ($140,000), with any broker or referral fees paid separately by the borrower
  • Full prepayment permitted at any time, with a 1% fee applying only if the loan was repaid within the first nine months of the term
  • Loan proceeds used to refinance the existing debt on the property and provide cash out specifically to complete the entitlement process for the approved project
  • A personal guaranty from the principal, alongside a $5,000 non-refundable deposit credited toward closing fees and standard due diligence requirements: financial statements, credit and background review, title and survey review, environmental review, and a review of all leases encumbering the property
  • Estimated closing expenses including a $3,000 documentation fee, $10,000 in title costs, $500 in recording fees, $3,000 in due diligence costs, and a $7,500 appraisal fee, all borne by the borrower regardless of whether the loan closed

The bridge loan closed with proceeds retiring the existing debt and freeing up the cash needed to carry the entitlement process to completion, keeping the site's development timeline on track without forcing a premature move to construction financing.

Why We Won This One

Finding a private capital lender genuinely comfortable underwriting against entitlement value on a non-income-producing site, rather than requiring a stabilized cash flow story, is what made this bridge possible at this loan size. A private bridge lender's willingness to size the loan against the strength of the approved Vesting Tentative Tract Map, rather than a rent roll that didn't exist yet, unlocked liquidity the ownership needed well before the project reached vertical construction.

Structuring the loan to deliver real cash-out proceeds specifically for entitlement completion, not just a straight debt-for-debt refinance, also mattered here. Isolating those costs clearly in the use-of-proceeds language kept the loan's purpose unambiguous to the lender's underwriting team and avoided any confusion about what the additional proceeds beyond the existing payoff were actually funding.

If you're holding entitled land and need liquidity to complete the entitlement process or bridge to construction financing, a private capital lender comfortable underwriting against approved entitlements rather than in-place cash flow can unlock proceeds a conventional bank won't offer.
