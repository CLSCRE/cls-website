Capital Markets Report: Week of July 27, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, and lender appetite.

Rate Environment

Commercial mortgage rates held in a narrow range during the week of July 27-31, 2026. The 10-Year Treasury closed the week near 4.33%.

Current Rate Ranges (as of late July 2026)

  • Agency Multifamily (Fannie/Freddie) 10-year fixed: ~5.55%, 6.10%
  • CMBS Conduit: ~6.00%, 6.65%
  • Life Company (stabilized): ~5.70%, 6.30%
  • Bridge / Transitional (floating): ~7.05%, 8.90% all-in (SOFR + 275 to 450 bps)

Key Takeaways

  • Markets are pricing roughly a 50/50 chance of a 25 bp Fed cut at the September 16-17 meeting.
  • Life companies remain constructive on long-term fixed-rate deals with strong sponsors.
  • Debt funds are competitive on transitional and value-add bridge structures.
  • Agency execution continues to set the benchmark for stabilized multifamily.

Lender Appetite Snapshot

Life companies, CMBS, Agency, banks/credit unions, and debt funds are all active but selective. The best execution continues to go to well-capitalized sponsors with clean track records and strong submarkets.

CLS CRE Perspective

Rates are stable but the market remains bifurcated. Borrowers with strong deals in industrial, multifamily, and select necessity retail are seeing competitive terms. Office and more challenged asset types require more creative structuring.
