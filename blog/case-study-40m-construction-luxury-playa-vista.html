Case Study

$40M Luxury Apartments (110 Units) in Playa Vista, Los Angeles, CA

Trevor Damyan Founder & Principal Broker
CA DRE #02244836

Former CBRE and Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation. $1B+ in closed commercial real estate financing across bridge, construction, permanent, and structured debt.

April 05, 2026·Construction Loan

The Deal

A prominent real estate development group approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking $40 million in construction financing for a luxury apartment project in Playa Vista, Los Angeles. The proposed development would deliver 110 high-end residential units strategically positioned near Silicon Beach, the technology hub that houses major companies including Google, Facebook, and numerous emerging startups.

The developer had identified a unique opportunity to serve the growing population of technology professionals who commanded high salaries but faced limited housing options within reasonable commuting distance of their workplaces. The project featured premium amenities including rooftop lounges, fitness facilities, co-working spaces, and smart home technology integration designed to appeal to tech-savvy residents.

Located in one of Los Angeles's most desirable submarkets, the development site offered proximity to both employment centers and lifestyle amenities. The sponsor brought significant experience in luxury residential development and had successfully completed similar projects in competitive urban markets.

The Challenge

Despite the project's attractive location and target demographic, multiple lenders expressed concerns about new apartment supply entering the Playa Vista market. Several competing developments were already under construction or in planning phases, creating uncertainty about absorption rates and rental pricing power.

The financing market had become increasingly cautious about multifamily construction loans, particularly for luxury products in markets experiencing rapid development activity. Lenders were demanding more conservative loan-to-cost ratios and requiring extensive market analysis to justify their underwriting assumptions.

Initial conversations with traditional construction lenders resulted in either declined proposals or financing terms that would have compromised the project's feasibility. The developer needed a financing partner who could look beyond surface-level market concerns and understand the underlying demand drivers supporting the luxury rental market near Silicon Beach.

Time constraints added additional pressure, as construction costs were escalating and the developer had already invested significant capital in site preparation and permitting activities.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan recognized that this deal required a comprehensive market analysis to address lender concerns about new supply absorption. Rather than accepting the common narrative about oversupply, he conducted detailed research into comparable luxury apartment deliveries in the greater Los Angeles area, focusing specifically on projects targeting similar demographics.

The analysis revealed that recent luxury apartment developments serving technology workers had achieved absorption rates significantly faster than market averages. Projects offering similar amenity packages and location advantages had consistently achieved stabilized occupancy within 12 to 18 months of delivery, despite initial market skepticism.

Damyan compiled employment growth data from major technology companies in the Silicon Beach area, demonstrating sustained job creation and salary growth that supported premium rental rates. The research showed that the target demographic prioritized location and amenities over rental cost, creating pricing power for well-positioned luxury developments.

Armed with this market intelligence, Damyan approached a relationship lender who specialized in California multifamily construction. He presented a comprehensive financing package that addressed the lender's risk concerns while highlighting the project's competitive advantages.

The final financing structure included a 60% loan-to-cost ratio with a 26-month construction timeline, providing adequate capital for project completion while maintaining conservative leverage levels that satisfied lender requirements.

The Outcome

Commercial Lending Solutions successfully secured the $40 million construction loan, enabling the developer to commence construction on schedule. The financing terms provided sufficient capital while maintaining project feasibility through reasonable interest rates and flexible draw procedures.

The lender appreciated the thorough market analysis that differentiated this project from competing developments and provided confidence in the absorption projections. By demonstrating superior demand characteristics for the target demographic, Damyan overcame initial skepticism about market conditions.

The developer avoided construction delays that could have added millions in additional costs and lost the opportunity to capture favorable market timing. The project proceeded to construction with a financing structure that supported successful completion and lease-up.

This transaction demonstrated the value of comprehensive market research in commercial real estate financing. Rather than accepting conventional wisdom about market conditions, detailed analysis revealed underlying demand patterns that supported the project's success.

The financing closed within 90 days of initial application, representing exceptional execution speed for a construction loan of this magnitude. The developer has since returned to Commercial Lending Solutions for additional projects, establishing a valuable ongoing relationship.

The successful financing of this luxury apartment development reinforced the importance of understanding local market dynamics and demographic trends when evaluating multifamily construction opportunities in competitive urban markets.
