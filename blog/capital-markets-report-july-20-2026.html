Capital Markets Report: Week of July 20, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, and lender appetite.

Rate Environment

Commercial mortgage rates remained in a relatively stable band during the week of July 20-24, 2026. The market continues to show gradual improvement in lender engagement, but execution is highly deal-specific.

Current Rate Ranges (Week of July 20, 2026)

  • Multifamily (Agency, Fannie/Freddie): 5.85%, 6.40% fixed (10-year, 75% LTV, stabilized)
  • Multifamily (Bank/Portfolio Permanent): 6.25%, 6.90% fixed (5- or 7-year)
  • CMBS (Retail/Industrial/Office): 6.50%, 7.25% fixed (10-year)
  • Life Company (Core Stabilized): 6.10%, 6.75% fixed (10-25 year)
  • SBA 504 (Owner-Occupied): 6.40%, 7.00% fixed
  • Bridge/Transitional (Debt Fund): 8.50%, 10.50% floating (SOFR + spread)
  • Construction (Bank): 7.75%, 9.25% floating

Key Takeaways

  • Life companies are hitting allocation targets and active on high-quality permanent placements.
  • CMBS issuance is running ahead of 2025 levels.
  • Industrial and multifamily remain the most competitive sectors for financing.
  • Maturity wall pressure is building, $800B-$1T in CRE debt maturing through end of 2026.

Lender Appetite Snapshot

Life companies, CMBS conduits, Agency, banks/credit unions, and debt funds are all participating, but selectivity remains high. Strong sponsorship, realistic valuations, and clean documentation are winning the best terms.

Action Items

  • Stress-test deals at current rates before submitting.
  • Run multiple capital stacks, the best execution isn't always the obvious one.
  • Don't wait for rate cuts; execution certainty matters more.
  • Get lender packages current (rent rolls, T12s, photos).
