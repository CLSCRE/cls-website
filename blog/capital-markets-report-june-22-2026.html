Capital Markets Report: Week of June 22, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, lender behavior, and opportunities in the second half of 2026.

Rate Environment

As we cross into the second half of 2026, bank behavior is evolving slowly, CMBS issuance is ahead of last year, and the maturity wall is creating real transaction opportunities for well-capitalized sponsors.

Key Rate Ranges (Week of June 22, 2026)

  • Life Company / Long-term Fixed: Competitive on stabilized multifamily, industrial, and select assets.
  • CMBS Conduit: Tracking ahead of 2025 pace with tighter AAA spreads.
  • Bridge / Transitional: Debt funds more willing to engage on value-add and repositioning.
  • SBA 504 (Owner-User): ~5.85% on debenture tranche; bank first trust deed floating or short-term fixed.
  • Hard Money / Private Bridge: 9.50%-12.00%+ for time-sensitive or distressed situations.

Second Half 2026 Themes

  • Bank re-entry: Selective, relationship-driven, focused on stabilized assets and lower LTV.
  • CMBS momentum: Year-to-date issuance ~18% ahead of 2025.
  • Distressed resolution accelerating: The maturity wall is now active, attractive vintage for value-add buyers with dry powder.
  • Los Angeles note: Insurance market challenges (wildfire-adjacent properties) are adding friction and requiring earlier coverage commitments.

Action Items

  • If maturing in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027, begin lender outreach now (45-90+ day timelines).
  • Get clean, current financials (rent rolls, T12, rent comps).
  • Evaluate rate cap extensions on floating-rate loans.
  • Get real-time submarket lender appetite intelligence before pricing.
