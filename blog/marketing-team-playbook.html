Section 1: LinkedIn Post Series Mark's Task

Goal: A 14-week LinkedIn campaign targeting professional referral partners -- attorneys, CPAs, fiduciaries, receivers, and family offices. Each post positions Trevor as the go-to commercial real estate financing expert for that professional's specific client situations. Consistent weekly posting builds recognition and drives inbound referrals over time.

Reference page with all 14 posts:
clscre.com/blog/linkedin-post-series-professional-referral.html
All 14 posts are written and ready to copy on this page. Open it before each posting session.

How to Post Each Week -- Step by Step

  1. Log into LinkedIn as Trevor Damyan: linkedin.com/in/trevor-damyan-42636397
  2. Open the reference page above in a separate tab.
  3. Copy the post for the current week. Posts are numbered in order on the page.
  4. On LinkedIn, click "Start a post" at the top of the feed.
  5. Paste the text exactly as written. Do not edit, shorten, or reformat it.
  6. Do NOT attach an image. Text-only posts consistently outperform image posts for professional B2B content on LinkedIn. The algorithm gives more distribution to text-only posts in this niche.
  7. Post on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM Pacific Time. Avoid Mondays (too busy) and Fridays (low engagement).
  8. For the first 60 minutes after posting: check the post every 15 minutes and reply to every comment. LinkedIn's algorithm rewards rapid engagement -- this directly increases how many people see the post. Set a phone reminder.
  9. If a commenter asks a deal-specific question (about a specific loan type, property, or financing situation), tag @Trevor Damyan in your reply so he can respond personally. Don't try to answer deal questions yourself.
  10. At the end of each week: note in a shared doc which post was published, the exact date, and a summary of any comments or engagement. Trevor reviews this monthly.

The 6 Directories

  1. SEAK Expert Witness Directory (seak.com) -- Largest US expert witness directory. Heavily used by plaintiff and defense attorneys. Annual fee approximately $595. Requires headshot and CV upload.
  2. ExpertPages (expertpages.com) -- One of the highest-traffic free expert witness directories. Paid premium placement ($299 to $699/year) improves search ranking significantly. Start with the free listing if budget is a consideration.
  3. Expert Institute (expertinstitute.com) -- Used by AmLaw 100 and large regional law firms. No listing fee for experts (they charge the law firms). Submit a profile and their intake team contacts you to complete vetting. Have prior testimony types ready to discuss (case types, not names).
  4. National Register of Expert Witnesses (national-register.com) -- Long-established directory with strong Google search presence. Annual fee approximately $300 to $500. Structured field format; the profile text maps directly to their form.
  5. Jurify (jurify.com) -- AI-powered legal expert search platform used by growing numbers of mid-size and large law firms. Free and paid tiers available. The profile text on the reference page is written to train Jurify's matching algorithm -- paste it exactly as written.
  6. ForensisGroup (forensisgroup.com) -- Specialized in forensic and financial experts, strong California client base. Not a self-service directory; they match experts to cases and contact you when a match arises. Submit your CV and bio and expect a brief intake phone screen. No listing fee for experts.

Also Complete in the Same Session

Section 4: Weekly and Monthly Maintenance

These tasks keep the campaigns healthy and the content engine running. They are short -- the weekly check is 15 minutes, the monthly review is 30 minutes.

Weekly -- Every Monday -- 15 Minutes
  1. Log into Google Ads and check for any ads marked "Disapproved." If any appear, take a screenshot and flag it to Trevor immediately by email or text. Do not try to fix disapprovals yourself -- ad policy issues require Trevor's judgment.
  2. Check whether the weekly rate blog post published automatically. Visit clscre.com/blog/ and confirm there is a post dated the prior Monday with current rate commentary. If no new post appears, let Trevor know. The post script runs automatically but occasionally needs a manual trigger.
  3. Scroll through last week's LinkedIn post and reply to any comments that did not receive a response. Even a short acknowledgment ("Great question -- Trevor will follow up") closes the loop and keeps the thread active.
Monthly -- First Monday of Each Month -- 30 Minutes
  1. Google Ads report: In Google Ads, filter to the prior 30 days. Note which campaigns have the lowest cost-per-click (these are performing well). Flag any campaigns with zero conversions in 30 days -- send Trevor a note: "Campaign X has run for 30 days with zero conversions. Should we pause it or adjust targeting?" Do not pause campaigns yourself without Trevor's approval.
  2. LinkedIn engagement report: Review the past month's posts. Note which post got the most likes plus comments combined. Send Trevor a 2-sentence summary: "Best post this month was [topic] with [X] engagements. Consider building more content or ad targeting around [that professional audience]." This takes about 5 minutes and directly shapes strategy.
  3. Website spot-check: Click through 10 pages on clscre.com. Use the nav to move between sections. Submit a test inquiry on the contact form (use a personal email so Trevor can confirm it arrives). If anything looks broken, a form does not work, or a page has an error, screenshot it and send to Trevor immediately.
