Capital Markets Report: Week of July 13, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, and lender appetite.

Rate Environment

Commercial mortgage rates were stable to slightly tighter in key segments during the week of July 13–17, 2026. Agency and life company execution remained competitive for high-quality stabilized assets.

Current Rate Ranges (Week of July 13, 2026)

  • Multifamily – Agency (Fannie/Freddie): 5.50% – 6.10% fixed (10-year term)
  • HUD/FHA 223(f) Refinance: 5.20% – 5.60% fixed (35-year amortizing)
  • CMBS Conduit: 6.20% – 7.00% fixed (10-year)
  • Life Company (Core Stabilized): 5.75% – 6.40% fixed
  • Bank / Credit Union (Portfolio): 6.00% – 7.25% fixed or floating
  • Bridge / Debt Fund (Transitional/Value-Add): ~8.25% – 10.00% all-in (SOFR + 275–450 bps)
  • SBA 504 (Owner-Occupied): 5.80% – 6.20% on the debenture portion

Key Takeaways

  • The Fed cut timeline (likely starting September) remains the dominant variable for longer-term rates.
  • Industrial and stabilized multifamily continue to see the strongest lender competition.
  • Office financing remains highly selective except for trophy, medical office, and life sciences assets.
  • Maturity wall pressure is real — engage brokers early on loans maturing in the next 12–18 months.

Lender Appetite Snapshot

Agency and life companies are the most reliable for stabilized product. Debt funds are competitive on transitional deals. Banks are selective but relationship-driven execution can still work well for smaller loans.

Action Items

  • Review your maturity schedule now — don't wait for the notice.
  • Stress-test at current rates.
  • Explore bridge-to-agency structures for value-add multifamily.
  • Get current rate quotes for market intelligence even if not transacting immediately.
