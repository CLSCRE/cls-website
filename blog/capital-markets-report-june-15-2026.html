Capital Markets Report: Week of June 15, 2026

Commercial Lending Solutions weekly update on Treasury yields, commercial mortgage rates, transaction volume, and cap rate dynamics.

Rate Environment

Mid-June 2026 showed modest improvement in long-term fixed-rate pricing as Treasuries drifted lower. Agency and life company execution remained strong for qualified stabilized assets.

Key Rate Ranges (Week of June 15, 2026)

  • Agency Multifamily (Fannie/Freddie): 5.85%, 6.50% fixed (5-10 year)
  • Life Company: 5.95%, 6.60% fixed (preferred for stabilized multifamily & industrial)
  • CMBS Conduit: 6.25%, 7.00% fixed
  • Bank/Portfolio (5-Year Fixed): 6.00%, 6.75%
  • Bridge/Transitional: SOFR + 275-400 bps (~7.50%-8.85% all-in)
  • Construction (Floating): SOFR + 250-375 bps
  • SBA 504: Blended 6.00%, 6.75%

Market Outlook & Themes

  • Fed & PCE: Benign inflation prints could push the 10-Year back toward 4.20% or lower, supporting CRE pricing into fall.
  • Transaction Volume: Q1/Q2 2026 running meaningfully ahead of 2025, led by multifamily and industrial. Grocery-anchored retail seeing renewed lender interest.
  • Lender Competition: Life companies and agency lenders ahead on targets, good window for qualified stabilized deals.
  • Cap Rates vs Financing Costs: Negative leverage has narrowed but not closed. Buyers using more equity and conservative assumptions.

Action Items

  • Get rate quotes on deals inside 90 days, modest Treasury rally creates a favorable window.
  • Stress-test at 4.50% on the 10-Year (conservative underwriting).
  • Engage early on 2026 maturities.
  • Consider locking long-term fixed for 7-10 year holds rather than floating.
