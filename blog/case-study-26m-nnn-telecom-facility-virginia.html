$26M NNN Telecom Facility in Northern Virginia, VA

By Trevor Damyan·April 14, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions arranged a $26M permanent loan for a nnn telecom facility in Northern Virginia, VA. The property was leased to an operator in a specialized net-lease sector under a long-term absolute NNN lease structure. The tenant's business model produced strong unit-level economics with consistent revenue at this location, and the lease carried annual rent escalations providing income growth over the hold period. The borrower was a private investor seeking long-term fixed-rate financing to support a portfolio strategy centered on necessity-based, operationally essential real estate.

The Challenge

Specialized net-lease categories such as fitness centers, car washes, and telecom facilities present underwriting challenges for lenders unfamiliar with the sector because property value is closely tied to the operator's continued use of the space. Traditional lenders who underwrite primarily on dark value, the value if the tenant vacates, consistently under-appraised these assets relative to their actual market cap rates, resulting in lower LTV approvals and less competitive rate structures that did not reflect the assets' income quality.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan at Commercial Lending Solutions sourced a specialty net-lease lender with an active book in the relevant property category, whose appraisers and credit team were experienced with operator-dependent net-lease assets. The loan was structured at 60% of the income-approach value with a 10-year fixed rate and 25-year amortization. The lender's familiarity with the tenant's brand, unit economics, and lease renewal behavior allowed the underwriting to proceed without the discount to dark value that generalist lenders were applying, producing a meaningfully better LTV and rate outcome.

The Outcome

The financing closed at terms that reflected the asset's actual income quality rather than a discounted dark-value haircut, delivering a favorable rate and sufficient proceeds to meet the borrower's refinance objectives. The 10-year fixed term provided long-term rate certainty aligned with the expected hold period, and the amortization schedule produced strong coverage ratios comfortable to the lender. The borrower added the property to their core long-term portfolio with institutional financing in place, and the experience reinforced the importance of matching specialty asset classes with lenders who have established sector expertise and appropriate underwriting frameworks.
