Case Study

$35M Ground-Up Multifamily (92 Units) in Arts District, Los Angeles, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Construction Loan

The Deal

A seasoned real estate developer approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking $35 million in construction financing for an ambitious ground-up multifamily project in Los Angeles' vibrant Arts District. The proposed development would transform a former industrial site into a modern 92-unit residential complex, capitalizing on the neighborhood's ongoing transformation from warehouse district to trendy residential enclave.

The Arts District location presented significant advantages, including proximity to downtown Los Angeles, excellent public transportation access, and a thriving cultural scene that continues to attract young professionals and creatives. The developer's vision called for contemporary design elements that would complement the area's industrial heritage while providing modern amenities expected by today's renters.

Initial project parameters included a 24-month construction timeline and a loan-to-cost ratio target of 60%. The developer brought substantial experience in urban infill projects and had successfully completed several similar developments in emerging Los Angeles neighborhoods over the previous decade.

The Challenge

The project's industrial site history created immediate complications when Phase I environmental assessments revealed contamination requiring extensive remediation. The environmental issues added an unexpected $2 million to the project budget, pushing total development costs significantly higher than originally projected.

Traditional construction lenders expressed reluctance to finance the project due to the environmental remediation requirements. Many institutions were uncomfortable with the uncertainty surrounding cleanup timelines and the potential for additional contamination discovery during excavation. The phased nature of environmental work, requiring cleanup completion before vertical construction could begin, created scheduling complexities that made lenders nervous about timeline extensions and cost overruns.

The borrower faced additional challenges in the form of strict city requirements for environmental remediation documentation and ongoing monitoring throughout construction. Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety mandated specific protocols for soil handling, air quality monitoring, and waste disposal that would impact both timeline and budget.

Market conditions added another layer of complexity, as construction costs were rising rapidly and labor availability remained constrained. The borrower needed to secure financing quickly to lock in contractor pricing while simultaneously managing the environmental remediation process.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan at Commercial Lending Solutions leveraged his extensive network to identify a regional bank with significant experience in environmental remediation projects. The selected lender had previously financed similar brownfield redevelopment projects and understood the unique challenges associated with former industrial sites.

The financing structure accommodated the project's phased approach through a carefully structured draw schedule. Initial funding covered Phase II environmental assessment and remediation work, with subsequent draws tied to specific environmental milestones rather than traditional construction benchmarks. This approach provided the lender with confidence that environmental issues would be fully addressed before major construction expenditures began.

Commercial Lending Solutions coordinated with environmental consultants, city officials, and the construction team to develop realistic timeline projections that accounted for both remediation requirements and seasonal construction considerations. The 24-month construction period included buffer time for potential environmental complications while maintaining aggressive timelines for the vertical construction phase.

The final financing package maintained the targeted 60% loan-to-cost ratio despite the increased budget, requiring the developer to contribute additional equity to cover environmental costs. Interest-only payments during construction helped manage cash flow during the remediation phase when no rental income was being generated.

The Outcome

The $35 million construction loan closed successfully with terms that protected both borrower and lender interests. The phased funding approach proved instrumental in managing environmental risks while maintaining project momentum. Environmental remediation was completed on schedule and within budget, allowing vertical construction to proceed without delays.

The project's location in the Arts District proved prescient as the neighborhood continued its rapid transformation during the construction period. Pre-leasing activity began six months before completion, with initial rental rates exceeding original projections by 8%. The building's modern design and amenity package resonated strongly with target demographics, generating significant leasing interest.

Construction was completed within the original 24-month timeline despite environmental complications, demonstrating the effectiveness of the phased approach and careful project management. The developer successfully navigated all city requirements and environmental monitoring protocols without significant issues.

The project achieved stabilized occupancy within four months of completion, with rental rates supporting refinancing into permanent financing. The successful completion enhanced the developer's reputation for complex urban infill projects and positioned them for additional opportunities in similar markets.

This transaction exemplifies Commercial Lending Solutions' ability to structure creative financing solutions for challenging development projects, turning potential obstacles into manageable project phases through careful lender selection and innovative deal structure.
