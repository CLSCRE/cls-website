$25M Office-to-Residential Conversion in Mid-Wilshire, Los Angeles, CA

By Trevor Damyan·April 05, 2026·Bridge Loan

The Deal

A seasoned real estate development group approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking $25 million in bridge financing to acquire a Class B office building in Mid-Wilshire, Los Angeles. The borrower's vision was ambitious: convert the existing office structure into 80 modern residential units to meet the growing demand for urban housing in one of LA's most desirable submarkets.

The property, located in the heart of Mid-Wilshire's bustling corridor, presented an ideal candidate for residential conversion. The building's solid bones, generous floor plates, and prime location offered significant potential for transformation into contemporary living spaces. However, the deal required immediate acquisition financing before the lengthy entitlement and conversion process could begin.

With no existing rental income and an exit strategy entirely dependent on successful entitlements and construction completion, this transaction demanded a lender with deep expertise in Los Angeles office-to-residential conversions and an appetite for pre-development risk.

The Challenge

The financing request presented several complex hurdles that eliminated most traditional lending sources. The property generated zero income, requiring a lender comfortable with asset-based underwriting rather than cash flow analysis. The borrower needed to close within 45 days to secure the acquisition, leaving little time for lengthy institutional loan processes.

Perhaps most significantly, the entire business plan hinged on obtaining residential conversion entitlements from the City of Los Angeles. The entitlement process, while supported by recent municipal initiatives encouraging office-to-residential conversions, remained uncertain and could extend 12 to 18 months. Any delays or denials would fundamentally impact the project's viability and exit strategy.

The borrower also required maximum leverage to preserve capital for the upcoming construction phase. With construction costs for residential conversions averaging $200 to $300 per square foot in Los Angeles, maintaining liquidity was crucial for project success.

Traditional bank lenders were unwilling to finance a non-income producing property with such regulatory uncertainty. CMBS lenders required stabilized cash flows. Even hard money lenders typically preferred projects with clearer exit paths and shorter hold periods.

The Solution

Trevor Damyan identified a private lending group with extensive experience in Los Angeles office-to-residential conversion projects. This lender had successfully financed similar transformations throughout the city and understood both the risks and rewards of pre-development bridge financing in this specialized niche.

The lender structured a $25 million bridge loan at 60% loan-to-value, providing the borrower with sufficient leverage while maintaining a conservative basis in the underlying real estate. The 11% interest rate reflected the pre-development risk profile while remaining competitive for this asset class and hold period.

The 24-month initial term provided adequate runway for the entitlement process, with built-in extension options should municipal approvals require additional time. The lender's familiarity with LA's Department of City Planning processes enabled realistic timeline expectations and milestone-based funding structures.

Interest-only payments minimized carrying costs during the non-income producing hold period. The loan structure included provisions for converting to construction financing upon successful entitlement, providing a seamless transition to the next development phase.

The lender's underwriting focused heavily on the borrower's track record with similar conversions, the property's physical suitability for residential use, and Mid-Wilshire's strong rental fundamentals supporting the ultimate business plan.

The Outcome

Commercial Lending Solutions successfully closed the $25 million bridge loan within the required 45-day timeframe, enabling the borrower to secure the office building acquisition. The streamlined private lending process eliminated the delays and documentation burdens that would have accompanied institutional financing.

The borrower immediately began the entitlement process, working with experienced land use attorneys and consultants familiar with LA's office-to-residential conversion procedures. The lender's understanding of typical timeline fluctuations provided flexibility as the project navigated municipal requirements.

Within eight months of closing, the borrower successfully obtained preliminary approvals for the 80-unit residential conversion. The Mid-Wilshire location's strong transportation access and urban amenities supported planning department approval and neighborhood acceptance of the increased density.

The financing positioned the borrower to capitalize on Los Angeles' growing office-to-residential conversion trend while providing the specialized lender with a profitable deployment of capital in their area of expertise. The transaction exemplified how experienced private lenders can bridge the gap between traditional financing limitations and innovative real estate strategies.

This successful collaboration demonstrated the value of matching complex development projects with lenders who possess specific market knowledge and risk tolerance for pre-development bridge financing in specialized property types.
