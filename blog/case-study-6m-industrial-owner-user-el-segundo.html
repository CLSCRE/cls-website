Case Study

$6,200,000 Industrial Owner-User in El Segundo, CA

Deal arranged and written by Trevor Damyan, Commercial Mortgage Broker
By Trevor Damyan · April 03, 2026 · Permanent Loan

The Deal

Commercial Lending Solutions recently closed a $6.2 million permanent financing package for an aerospace parts manufacturer acquiring an owner-user industrial facility in El Segundo. The borrower, a well-established manufacturing company with consistent revenue streams, needed to relocate from their existing facility to accommodate business expansion in the highly competitive aerospace supply chain market. The 85,000 square foot industrial property sits within El Segundo's coveted aerospace and defense corridor, positioned strategically near LAX for logistics advantages. The borrower's business model centers on precision manufacturing for commercial and defense aerospace applications, requiring specialized facility configurations that make relocation decisions particularly complex and costly. With industrial vacancy rates in El Segundo hovering near historic lows and available facilities rarely exceeding 60 days on market, the borrower faced significant time pressure to secure both the real estate and corresponding financing package.

The Challenge

The primary financing obstacle centered on the borrower's financial profile, which exhibited strong top-line revenue performance but characteristically thin profit margins typical of contract manufacturing operations. While annual revenues exceeded $15 million with consistent growth trajectories, EBITDA margins remained compressed due to competitive pricing pressures and capital-intensive production requirements. The borrower's existing banking relationship initially appeared promising, with the regional bank expressing willingness to provide conventional acquisition financing. However, the proposed terms reflected the lender's conservative approach to manufacturing credits: a 75% loan-to-value ratio requiring 25% down payment, combined with an interest rate exceeding 7.5% based on current market conditions and perceived industry risk. Additionally, the conventional structure would have required the borrower to inject approximately $1.55 million in cash equity, significantly impacting working capital availability during a critical expansion phase. The high debt service burden from the quoted rate would have further constrained cash flow precisely when the business needed maximum financial flexibility for equipment installation and operational ramp-up. The compressed timeline added another layer of complexity, as El Segundo's industrial market moves rapidly and the borrower risked losing their preferred facility to competing offers while exploring financing alternatives.

The Solution

Commercial Lending Solutions structured an SBA 504 financing package that addressed both the borrower's capital preservation needs and rate sensitivity concerns. The solution involved a two-loan structure: a conventional first mortgage covering 50% of the project cost, paired with an SBA 504 Certified Development Company second mortgage financing 40% of the acquisition. The conventional first mortgage, placed with a regional bank experienced in SBA transactions, carried a 10-year fixed rate term at 6.25% with a 25-year amortization schedule. The CDC second mortgage provided 25-year fixed-rate financing at the current SBA 504 debenture rate of 5.15%, creating a blended rate of approximately 5.7%. This structure reduced the borrower's required equity injection from 25% to 10%, lowering the cash requirement from $1.55 million to approximately $620,000. The equity savings allowed the borrower to retain over $900,000 in working capital while simultaneously reducing their overall cost of capital by nearly 200 basis points compared to the conventional alternative. The SBA 504 program's owner-occupancy requirements aligned perfectly with the borrower's intended use, while the job creation and retention aspects supported the application's approval through the local Certified Development Company. We coordinated closely with both the conventional lender and CDC to ensure simultaneous closing capability within the required timeframe.

The Outcome

The transaction closed within 45 days of initial application, meeting the borrower's timeline requirements despite the multi-lender coordination complexity. The borrower successfully acquired their target facility while preserving substantial working capital for their expansion initiatives. The blended interest rate of 5.7% generated approximately $175,000 in annual debt service savings compared to the conventional alternative, improving cash flow coverage ratios and providing additional operational flexibility. The 10% equity requirement enabled the company to maintain adequate liquidity reserves for equipment purchases and inventory buildup associated with their facility transition. From a market perspective, the transaction demonstrates the continued viability of SBA 504 financing for credit-worthy manufacturing businesses, particularly in high-value industrial markets where equity preservation becomes critical. The structure's success also reflects growing lender comfort with aerospace supply chain credits, despite margin pressures affecting the broader manufacturing sector. The borrower has since completed their facility transition ahead of schedule and expanded production capacity by approximately 40%, validating their growth projections and reinforcing the financing structure's effectiveness in supporting business expansion objectives within capital-intensive industries. This transaction exemplifies how strategic capital stack optimization can transform challenging financing scenarios into competitive advantages, particularly for businesses operating in supply-constrained real estate markets with time-sensitive acquisition requirements.
