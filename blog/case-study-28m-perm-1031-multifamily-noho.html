Case Study

$28M 1031 Exchange Multifamily (78 Units) in North Hollywood, Los Angeles, CA

Trevor Damyan Founder & Principal Broker
CA DRE #02244836

Former CBRE and Marcus & Millichap Capital Corporation. $1B+ in closed commercial real estate financing across bridge, construction, permanent, and structured debt.

April 05, 2026·Permanent Loan

The Deal

A sophisticated real estate investor approached Commercial Lending Solutions seeking permanent financing for a 78-unit multifamily acquisition in North Hollywood, Los Angeles. The transaction represented a strategic shift from industrial holdings to residential income property through a 1031 exchange structure. The $28 million purchase required careful coordination between the exchange timeline and permanent loan execution to preserve significant tax advantages while securing favorable long-term financing. The target property consisted of well-maintained apartment units in a prime Los Angeles submarket known for strong rental demand and stable occupancy rates. The investor recognized the opportunity to diversify their portfolio while leveraging the 1031 exchange mechanism to defer substantial capital gains from the sale of their industrial property. The transaction required seamless execution to meet strict exchange deadlines while securing institutional-quality permanent financing.

The Challenge

The primary challenge centered on the compressed timeline inherent in 1031 exchange transactions. The property was identified on day 40 of the 45-day identification period, leaving minimal buffer time before the 180-day exchange deadline. With closing required within 60 days of identification to complete the exchange successfully, traditional financing timelines would not accommodate the accelerated schedule. The borrower faced potential forfeiture of approximately $3 million in capital gains tax benefits if the exchange failed to close within the required timeframe. Standard permanent loan processing typically requires 75 to 90 days, creating a significant timing gap that threatened the entire transaction structure. Additionally, the transition from industrial to multifamily property required comprehensive due diligence and underwriting analysis within the condensed timeline. Market conditions added complexity as lenders maintained stringent underwriting standards while processing volumes remained elevated. The borrower required certainty of execution rather than simply competitive pricing, as any financing delays would result in exchange failure and immediate tax consequences far exceeding any interest rate savings from alternative financing sources.

The Solution

Commercial Lending Solutions implemented a pre-staged financing strategy designed specifically for time-sensitive 1031 exchange transactions. Upon initial contact, the team immediately ordered all required third-party reports including appraisal, environmental assessment, and property condition analysis before formal loan application submission. This parallel processing approach eliminated sequential delays that typically extend closing timelines. The financing team leveraged established relationships with Fannie Mae execution partners to expedite underwriting review and approval processes. Pre-qualification of the borrower occurred simultaneously with property due diligence, allowing immediate submission of complete loan packages once purchase agreements were executed. All documentation requirements were anticipated and prepared in advance to avoid common delays during the approval process. Communication protocols ensured daily coordination between all transaction participants including the exchange intermediary, title company, and loan processor. Regular status updates provided transparency while proactive issue identification allowed immediate resolution of potential obstacles. The team structured the loan terms to align with the borrower's long-term investment strategy while meeting Fannie Mae underwriting requirements for institutional multifamily properties. Risk mitigation included backup documentation preparation and alternative execution strategies should unexpected delays emerge. The financing team maintained direct communication channels with decision makers at each stage to ensure priority processing and immediate attention to any emerging issues that could threaten the exchange timeline.

The Outcome

The permanent loan successfully closed on day 58 of the 60-day target timeline, completing the 1031 exchange within required deadlines and preserving the full $3 million in capital gains tax savings. Fannie Mae provided favorable long-term financing terms appropriate for the quality asset and experienced borrower profile. The two-day buffer provided confidence throughout the closing process while demonstrating the effectiveness of the pre-staged financing approach. The borrower achieved their strategic objective of transitioning from industrial to multifamily real estate while maintaining tax-deferred status on their accumulated gains. The North Hollywood property provides stable cash flow with potential for appreciation in the strong Los Angeles rental market. Long-term financing terms support the investment strategy while preserving capital for additional acquisitions. The successful execution established a framework for future 1031 exchange transactions requiring similar timeline compression and coordination complexity. The borrower expressed satisfaction with both the financing terms and the execution certainty provided throughout the process. The transaction demonstrates how specialized commercial lending expertise can overcome timing constraints that might otherwise prevent beneficial real estate exchanges. Commercial Lending Solutions' proactive approach and established lender relationships proved essential in delivering results within the non-negotiable timeline requirements. The outcome reinforced the value of working with experienced commercial lending professionals who understand the unique requirements and timing pressures associated with 1031 exchange transactions in competitive real estate markets.
